Eldritch Battery has three effects: skill mana costs are paid from energy shield first, energy shield protects mana instead of life, and energy shield recharge rate is 50% less. It is not a conversion: the skill still has a mana cost that mana-cost modifiers change, only the pool that pays it changes. For defence it only works together with a Mana-before-Life source (reference id mind-over-matter): without one, energy shield no longer takes any hit, and with Chaos Inoculation the build dies to the first hit (probe below). It also stops the mana-spent mechanics (Arcane Surge, Inspiration), because energy shield spent does not count as mana spent.

Pipeline: [[Receiving damage#Energy shield]] and [[Receiving damage#Mana before life]] - energy shield is taken before the resource it protects; Eldritch Battery moves that resource from life to mana, so a hit reaches energy shield only through the Mana-before-Life share
