The Juggernaut ascendancy (tree group of start node 17765) is the Marauder's armour, endurance charge and stun-immunity ascendancy: seven notables in 3.29, each with its own small armour node in front. This note lists every notable with its tree text, its wiki rule, how PoB parses it (live parse through PoB's own tree pipeline) and what PoB does not count. It is SOURCE-ONLY: no build among the user's builds is a Juggernaut (scan by ascendClassName found 0), so there is no measured table and no PoB delta in this note. Live-parse grades: 5 full (Unbreakable, Undeniable, Unrelenting, Unstoppable, Untiring) and 2 partial (Unflinching 1 of 4 lines applied, Unyielding 3 of 4). The tree text of all seven notables, and of every other node of the ascendancy, is identical in 3_26, 3_27, 3_28 and 3_29 (script comparison in the evidence), so the 3_29 parse holds for older trees too. Mechanics that have their own note are linked, not repeated: [[Armour and physical max hit]] and [[Iron Reflexes]] (Unbreakable), [[Accuracy and hit chance]] (Undeniable), [[Endurance charges]] (Unflinching, Unrelenting, Unyielding), [[Stun]], [[Chill]] and [[Freeze]] (Unstoppable), [[Recovery]] and [[Flat physical damage reduction]] (Untiring, Unrelenting), [[Receiving damage]], [[Damage calculation]].

Pipeline: [[Receiving damage#Armour and damage reduction]] - Unbreakable's doubled body armour and its armour-also-applies-to-chaos line, Unrelenting's per-charge damage reduction; [[Receiving damage#Immunity and avoidance]] - Unstoppable's stun immunity and its action-speed and movement-speed floors; [[Receiving damage#Recovery]] - Untiring's regeneration rate and its recoup of prevented physical damage; [[Damage calculation#Hit chance]] - Undeniable's accuracy; [[Damage calculation#Hit rate]] - Undeniable's attack speed per accuracy; [[Damage calculation#Increased and reduced]] - Unyielding's damage and area per Endurance Charge.

Small nodes (tree.lua stats, each also gives 15% increased Armour): 32115 5% increased Attack Speed; 51998 +11% to Chaos Resistance; 49153 and 23972 15% increased Endurance Charge Duration each; 63417 4% increased Movement Speed; 16093 Regenerate 0.5% of Life per second; 62349 20% increased Stun Duration on Enemies. Unrelenting 56789 has an empty `in` list and lists 23972 in `out`, and 23972 lists Unflinching 1734 in `in` (tree.lua:15353, 15379, 15316), so it is reached through 23972 behind Unflinching; the evidence file calls it the root of its own branch, which the lists do not show.

Unbreakable 53816 (tree.lua:15298, name line 15300, stats 15305-15306): "Armour from Equipped Body Armour is doubled", "15% of Armour also applies to Chaos Damage taken from Hits". Wiki rev 1483887: it doubles the armour received from the equipped body armour; chaos resistance applies first, then the armour reduction; with Iron Reflexes the evasion converted from the body armour is doubled; the 15% line gives no extra chaos reduction with The Fourth Vow. Live parse: Armour MORE 100 on the body armour slot with a Multiplier ArmourDoubled limit, and ArmourAppliesToChaosDamageTaken 15 (CalcDefence.lua:112, 2011); grade full. PoB does not count (not run, unverified): the Iron Reflexes converted-evasion doubling (open item in Iron Reflexes.md) and the Fourth Vow exception.

Undeniable 44297 (tree.lua:15334, name line 15336, stats 15341-15343): "+500 to Accuracy Rating", "1% increased Attack Speed per 150 Accuracy Rating", "Gain Accuracy Rating equal to twice your Strength". Wiki rev 1418915 (3.13.0 now per 150, twice Strength, +500). Live parse: Accuracy BASE 500, Speed INC 1 per 150 Accuracy (attacks), Accuracy BASE 2 per Strength; grade full, no gate and no cap on the attack speed line. PoB does not count anything missing here; its value is build-specific (needs Strength and an attack skill) and the hit-chance part only matters below 100% hit chance, see [[Accuracy and hit chance]].

Unflinching 1734 (tree.lua:15353, name line 15355, stats 15360-15363, reminder 15366 "Recently refers to the past 4 seconds"): "30% chance to gain an Endurance Charge when you are Hit", "25% chance that if you would gain Endurance Charges, you instead gain up to maximum Endurance Charges", "Gain 1 Endurance Charge every second if you've been Hit Recently", "+1 to Maximum Endurance Charges". Wiki rev 1418921 (3.2.0 added the 'instead gain up to maximum' line and the Hit Recently line, 3.0.0 hit chance 30%). Live parse: only line 4 applies (EnduranceChargesMax BASE 1); line 1 is unparsed (ModCache.lua:8140), lines 2 and 3 parse to junk EnduranceChargesMax mods with leftover text and are dropped (ModCache.lua:7222-7224 and 15646-15647; Classes/PassiveTree.lua:860-862 adds a line's mods only when `not mod.extra`); grade partial. PoB does not count: all three charge-generation lines; the charge count is a hand-set config value (useEnduranceCharges, ConfigOptions.lua:912, overrideEnduranceCharges :915, off = 0 charges), so the +1 maximum matters only once the box is ticked.

Unrelenting 56789 (tree.lua:15379, name line 15381, stats 15386-15387): "1% additional Elemental Damage Reduction per Endurance Charge", "1% additional Physical Damage Reduction per Endurance Charge". Wiki rev 1463604 (3.25.0 replaced the chaos resistance and the elemental damage taken lines with these). Live parse: ElementalDamageReduction BASE 1 and PhysicalDamageReduction BASE 1, each per Endurance Charge (CalcDefence.lua:1538); grade config (user grading rule 2026-10-26: parsed fully, 0% until useEnduranceCharges is ticked). Gate: the Endurance Charge multiplier is 0 at the defaults; with the 4 maximum charges Unflinching allows it is at most 4% physical and 4% elemental. The physical line is a flat damage-reduction term summed with the armour reduction under the 90% cap, see [[Flat physical damage reduction]].

Unstoppable 5819 (tree.lua:15278, name line 15280, stats 15285-15288): "10% increased Movement Speed", "Cannot be Stunned", "Action Speed cannot be modified to below Base Value", "Movement Speed cannot be modified to below Base Value". Wiki rev 1550263: the action speed floor means Chill, Freeze and Temporal Chains are ignored unless something raises speed above base; the movement floor ignores tarred ground and immobilise. Live parse: MovementSpeed INC 10, StunImmune, MinimumActionSpeed MAX 100 (CalcPerform.lua:1143), MovementSpeedCannotBeBelowBase (CalcDefence.lua:1544); grade full, no gate. PoB counts the floor in the player's action speed; the effect on a boss preset's slows was not measured. See [[Stun]] (lists this node), [[Chill]], [[Freeze]] (the floor is not yet in those notes).

Untiring 17988 (tree.lua:15203, name line 15205, stats 15210-15211): "40% increased Life Regeneration rate", "1.5% of Physical Damage prevented from Hits in the past\n10 seconds is Regenerated as Life per second" (one string with an embedded newline). Wiki rev 1493167 (added 3.19.0). Live parse: LifeRegen INC 40; PoB's line-combining step joins the two halves into PhysicalDamageMitigatedLifePseudoRecoup 15 with Duration 10 (CalcDefence.lua:1494-1503); grade full. PoB does not count the recoup in TotalEHP: it is an output pseudo-value only. No vault note covers the 'prevented damage' mechanic (candidate id prevented-damage-recoup).

Unyielding 62595 (tree.lua:15454, name line 15456, stats 15461-15464): "8% increased Damage per Endurance Charge", "6% increased Area of Effect per Endurance Charge", "25% chance to gain an Endurance Charge when you Stun an Enemy", "10% increased Stun Duration on Enemies per Endurance Charge". Wiki rev 1418920 (3.7.0 8% damage and 6% area; Unflinching is its prerequisite). Live parse: lines 1, 2 and 4 apply per Endurance Charge (Damage INC, AreaOfEffect INC, EnemyStunDuration INC); line 3 is unparsed (ModCache.lua:7323-7324); grade partial. At 4 charges the lines are worth 32% increased Damage, 24% increased Area of Effect and 40% increased Stun Duration, arithmetic from the node text, not run. PoB does not count the charge gain on stun.

Not measured: no sample, so there is no PoB delta, no TotalDPS or TotalEHP number and no config state in this note. Every figure above is the tree text, the wiki quote or PoB source; none is a game fact beyond the wiki rule cited.

On-hit and when-hit sources of this ascendancy (rule from task 027): (1) Unflinching 'chance to gain an Endurance Charge when you are Hit' and 'Gain 1 every second if you've been Hit Recently': a when-hit source on the character, not a hit of the player's; the hit rate is the boss's or the monsters' rate on the player and PoB has no output field for it, so the vault assumption of 1 boss hit per second (Pantheon and bandits.md:116, user 2026-10-25) is the only input; the Hit Recently window is 4 s (tree.lua:15366); PoB's box for the charges is useEnduranceCharges and is not ticked by default, and PoB drops both lines. (2) Unyielding 'chance to gain an Endurance Charge when you Stun an Enemy': on the player's stuns, unparsed, hit rate and stun rate not available (no sample). The vault 'Chance on hit uptime' model needs a PoB hit rate, so these uptimes are NOT VERIFIED as numbers.
