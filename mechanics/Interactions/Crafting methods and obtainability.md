A rare's mods come from a method, and the method decides what is guaranteed, what is reweighted and what the item cost to make. PoB counts every mod line on an item the same way (Item.lua flags crafted, custom, fractured lines but does not weigh them), so it can say what a line is worth but never how hard the line was to get. The generator prices a rare with rare_chance (weight / pool total per mod, exact set chance); that number covers only the natural mods. Essence and bench mods are removed by the callers (gen_validate.py:355, gen_gear_pool.py:265, tools/gen_rare_chances.py:117), and every other method below is not modelled at all.
Seed finding: of 18 `{crafted}` explicit entries on the equipped seed rares, 12 read as ModMaster rows tagged unveiled_mod (veiled/Jun crafts) that the generator's bench set excludes (gen_mods.py bench_crafts), 5 are plain bench rows and 1 is the multimod meta craft.

Pipeline: none - crafting acts before the pipeline: it decides which mod lines exist on the item; PoB then counts those lines in the normal steps ([[Damage calculation]], [[Receiving damage]]). See [[Radius jewels]] and [[Cluster jewels]] for jewels, [[Abyss jewels]] for the abyss rares in the seeds.
