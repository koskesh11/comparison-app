The Guardian ascendancy (Templar, tree start node 409) is the Templar's aura, block and sentinel ascendancy: nine notables in 3.29, eight on the tree plus the hidden Searing Purity 57568, an orphan node with no links that is granted only through matching Forbidden Flame and Forbidden Flesh jewels ([[Forbidden Flame and Flesh]]). Two are new in 3.28.0 (Marshal of Divinity and its small node Hallowing Flame Effect), and Time of Need changed text in 3.28.0 ("Curses and Elemental Ailments" became "Curses and Ailments"). This note lists every notable with its tree text, its wiki rule, how PoB parses it (live parse through PoB's own tree pipeline: 8 full, Time of Need partial) and, separately, what PoB does with the parsed mods. The two grades differ for two notables: Radiant Faith parses on every line but its mod GrantReservedManaAsAura has no reader anywhere in PoB, so it adds 0 Armour and 0 Energy Shield; Marshal of Divinity's second line (Multiplier:HallowingFlameMax) has no reader either. Grade these by effect, not by parse. Mechanics that have their own note are linked, not repeated: [[Block]] (Bastion of Hope), [[Minions]] and [[Minion alive count]] (Radiant Crusade, Unwavering Crusade), [[Receiving damage]] and [[Defences]] (the Sentinel's damage redirect), [[Herald of Ash, Zealotry and Anger]] (the relic's Anger aura is another provider), [[Chance on hit uptime]] and [[Trigger rate cap and cooldown]] (the relic trigger), [[Conversion and gain as extra]] (Hatred, Marshal's extra lightning), [[Aura effect and aura count]], [[Aura-conditional uniques]] and [[Reservation and reservation efficiency]] (Radiant Faith, Unwavering Faith), [[Recovery]], [[Elemental ailment immunity]] and [[Curse application and effect]] (Time of Need), [[Max resistances]] and [[Map modifiers]] (shrines), [[Physical damage taken as elemental]] and [[Chaos Inoculation]] (Searing Purity), [[Culling strike]] (sample G1's Full Culling part). The Scion's Ascendant tree can reach a reduced Guardian option, see [[Ascendant ascendancy]].

Pipeline: [[Receiving damage#Block]] and [[Defences#Block chance]] - Bastion of Hope's block for you and nearby allies; [[Defences#Effective HP]] - Radiant Crusade's 20% redirect to the Sentinel's life pool (the ally life pool list, Modules/CalcDefence.lua:33-45); [[Receiving damage#Damage taken as]] - Searing Purity; [[Receiving damage#Recovery]] - Time of Need's burst regeneration; [[Damage calculation#Gain as extra]] - Marshal of Divinity's extra lightning and the relic's Hatred aura; Unwavering Faith is a reservation term (see [[Reservation and reservation efficiency]]); Radiant Faith would act in the armour and energy shield totals, but PoB does not compute it.

On-hit and when-hit sources of this ascendancy (rule from task 027). (1) Unwavering Crusade, 25% chance to trigger the relic on a kill by you or an ally, or on a hit against a Rare or Unique. Source line above; hit rate from PoB output fields on sample G1: 6 spirits at m_Speed 15.389 per second and m_HitChance 100 = about 92 hits per second; relic duration 5 s, cooldown 0.3 s (wiki rev 1701315); PoB's config boxes for the three auras are ticked by default and the relic is always on. Uptime per scenario with the vault 'Chance on hit uptime' model u = 1 - (1 - p)^H, assuming a uniform element pick (p = 1/3 per trigger, not verified) and treating the minions as 'allies' (wiki wording, not game-tested). Pinnacle Boss: every hit on the unique boss is an eligible event, the 0.3 s cooldown binds so there is about one trigger per 0.3 s, up to 16 earlier triggers inside 5 s, each element up u = 1 - (2/3)^16 = 99.8% (arithmetic, not run); PoB's 100% matches. Mapping: eligible events are kills plus hits on rares and uniques, and PoB has no kill rate; with k eligible events per second (illustrative assumptions, Poisson refresh form 1 - exp(-k x 0.25/3 x 5)) the uptime per element is k = 1: 34%, k = 2: 56%, k = 5: 87%, k = 10: 98%; while a rare is being hit the boss figure approaches. Value when up: PoB delta on sample G1, single-enemy model, config as saved, not a game fact: all three auras -35.40% / -35.06% FullDPS (Summon Raging Spirit of Enormity and the derived Full Culling part), each box -10.6% to -13.1%; a lower uptime scales each box's share (the Mapping value at k = 2 is roughly 0.56 x 35% before interaction, hand reasoning, not measured). Vault rule: tick a box at u >= 80%, tick plus a notice at 50-80%, leave unticked with a warning below 50%. (2) Marshal of Divinity, Hallowing Flame on a melee hit: the player's hits are the source and sample G1's player does not hit (TotalDPS 0), so no p can be read from the sample; stack count and removal are hand-set boxes; NOT VERIFIED as a number. (3) Bastion of Hope, Time of Need and Harmony of Purpose are state or timer based, not chance based: Bastion needs a non-triggered attack within 4 s for the attack half (G1's Shield Charge is a manual attack in a group outside FullDPS) and a non-triggered spell for the spell half (SRS at 2.7 casts per second holds it); Time of Need is a 1 s window every 4 s (25% average); Harmony of Purpose gives each of up to 15 shrine types a rough 13% chance of being up at a moment if drawn uniformly (1 - (14/15)^2, the wiki weights are a small-sample unverified table), so ticking a specific shrine overcounts.
