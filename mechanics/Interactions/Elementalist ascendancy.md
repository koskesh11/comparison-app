The eight Elementalist notables (Witch ascendancy; 8 ascendancy points in total, each notable costs 2 points with its small node, Heart of Destruction costs 4 with the nodes in front of it, so a build gets four of the eight). Mastermind of Discord (exposure -25 extra, mana regeneration), Heart of Destruction (Convergence, area of effect), Bastion of Elements (Primal Aegis), Bringer of Ruin (herald effect and reservation), Shaper of Flames, Storms and Winter (guaranteed ignite, shock, chill; every damage type can cause them) and Liege of the Primordial (golems). Heart of Destruction sits behind Mastermind of Discord on the same branch (tree.lua: 37114 -> 61259 -> 19595 -> 57197), so taking Heart means taking Mastermind and two small nodes. Each other notable has its own two-node branch from the start node 16023 (the small node in front gives 10% increased Elemental Damage plus a second stat, so an "added notable" always brings that small node).

Both seeds that carry this ascendancy (Worb and the Fireball build) allocate the same four notables: Mastermind of Discord, Heart of Destruction, Shaper of Storms and Liege of the Primordial (class Witch, ascendancy Elementalist in both XML files; 8 ascendancy points used, ascUsed 8 in the headless runs). They skip Bastion of Elements, Bringer of Ruin, Shaper of Flames and Shaper of Winter. Links: [[Exposure]], [[Elemental Equilibrium]] (Mastermind stacks on it), [[Shock]], [[Ignite]], [[Chill]], [[Golem buffs]], [[Heralds]], [[Primal Aegis and Bastion of Elements]].

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#Resistance, exposure and penetration]] for Mastermind of Discord, [[Damage calculation#More and less]] for Convergence, [[Damage calculation#Ailments from hits]] and [[Damage calculation#Damage taken]] for the three Shapers, [[Receiving damage#Guard and Aegis]] for Bastion of Elements; Bringer of Ruin and Liege act through buffs (see the linked notes).

Wiki (only where it adds to the tree text): Heart of Destruction rev 1419042 and Convergence rev 1625601 - the 8 s is a cooldown between gains, Convergence lasts 4 s and stacks with Elemental Overload (so at most 4 s of every 8 s, and only against Unique enemies: map bosses and pinnacle bosses, never rare packs). Shaper of Storms rev 1537040 - the 25% minimum is a base value that shock and lightning ailment effect modifiers still scale. Shaper of Winter rev 1597914 - the damage reduction uses the strongest chill on the enemy, even one from another source, and does nothing against enemies unaffected by chill. Shaper of Flames rev 1593789 - the enemy conversion is real conversion (lowers physical damage dealt, also lets monsters ignite on crit with the new fire damage), not 'damage taken as'. Liege rev 1625326 - golem buffs are off while the golems are dead (the 4 s resummon is a gap, 3.28.0 removed the 50% golem buff effect). Mastermind rev 1438960 - the extra -25 is additive to the exposure's own value (Frost Bomb -15 becomes -40) and does not give the mana regeneration when a proxy (trap) applies it. Bastion rev 1579851 - disables the four unique Aegis skills. Bringer rev 1619829 - introduced 3.26.0 replacing Elemancer, no longer needs Liege.

On-hit uptime (project rule, [[Chance on hit uptime]] formula u = 1-(1-p)^n, n = ceil(hit rate x d) - 1 earlier hits, m = 1 hit per use as a lower bound, floored): Shaper of Storms and Flames set p = 100%, so the effect is up from the second hit on (always). Without Storms the seed's own shock chance gives Worb p = 25% (EnemyShockChance 25 after the dealloc), HitSpeed 6.45, ShockDuration 3.4 s, n = 21, u = 99%; Fireball seed p = 15%, Speed 5.03, ShockDuration 3 s, n = 15, u = 91%. So in Worb the notable adds no uptime, only the 25% floor (below the hand-set 57 in the box); in the Fireball seed it adds the floor and the last 9 points of uptime. Mastermind's mana regeneration is a 4 s 'Recently' window after an exposure application; PoB sets the condition whenever any exposure is on the enemy (CalcPerform.lua:3656), no timing. PoB's config box state: Worb shock/chill/cold exposure boxes are ticked (u is at least 99% at 100% chance), Convergence is unticked in both seeds.
