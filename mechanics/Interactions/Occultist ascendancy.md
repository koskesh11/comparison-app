The Witch's Occultist ascendancy has eight notables in 3.29 (tree.lua:44221-44530): Vile Bastion (ES), Void Beacon (enemy resistances), Forbidden Power (power charges), Withering Presence (chaos), Frigid Wake (cold), Profane Bloom (corpse explosion), Unholy Authority (curses) and the new Unhallowed Rite (Spirit Infusion). Each notable sits behind a minor node that gives energy shield plus one small stat, and two of them hang off another notable: Withering Presence and Frigid Wake are only reachable through Void Beacon, Unhallowed Rite needs Vile Bastion or Forbidden Power. Deallocating Void Beacon therefore drops Withering Presence and the minor next to it as well (PoB DeallocNode, Classes/PassiveSpec.lua:830-842). Other notes own the mechanics: [[Wither]], [[Freeze]], [[Power charges]], [[Curse limit]], [[Energy shield recovery keystones]], [[Chaos Inoculation]], [[Maim and Hinder]], [[Channelling]], [[Cast while Channelling]]; this note only says what each Occultist line does in PoB and what the notable is worth on the FR seed.

How PoB applies each line (ModCache.lua line; "full" only when every line yields mods):
- Vile Bastion: all three lines parse (ModCache.lua:19205 EnergyShieldRegen BASE 100; 14533 StunImmune FLAG under Condition HaveEnergyShield; 17644 flag EnergyShieldIncreasedByChanceToBlockSpellDamage). Consumer CalcDefence.lua:830-836 adds ES INC equal to output.SpellBlockChance. The stun immunity only exists when Condition:HaveEnergyShield is true, and grep of Modules/Classes finds that condition set only by the config box conditionHaveEnergyShield (ConfigOptions.lua:192-193; FR ticks it): with the box unticked PoB shows no stun immunity. Wiki (rev 1697427, 3.26.0 row): 100 ES per second and ES inc by spell block replaced the old per-kill regeneration.
- Void Beacon: all three lines parse as EnemyModifier LIST mods (ModCache.lua:18362 cold -20, 18361 chaos -20, 18378 life regeneration INC -100) applied to the enemy modDB in every run (CalcPerform.lua:625-633); no config gate and no radius. Wiki rev 1418984: the effect has a radius of 60 (forum post cited there); PoB has no distance, so 'nearby' always counts. Life regeneration -100% changes no PoB output (enemy life regeneration is not used).
- Forbidden Power: 3 of 4 lines parse (ModCache.lua:230 PowerChargesMax +1; 10700 AreaOfEffect INC 6 per PowerCharge; 10739 Damage INC 6 per PowerCharge); "Gain a Power Charge after Spending a total of 200 Mana" is unparsed (ModCache.lua:16217, second element nil) so no charge is generated. Charges only count when usePowerCharges is ticked (ConfigOptions.lua:900-902, FR ticks it), and then PoB assumes maximum (7). The Damage line is plain Damage, not spell damage (wiki prose says spell damage; its 3.13.0 row says Damage; tree.lua:44270-44275 says Damage).
- Withering Presence: all four lines parse (ModCache.lua:2415 ChaosResist +60; 5456 ChaosDamage MORE 15; 15493 flag Condition:CanWither; 18405 EnemyModifier DamageOverTime INC -15 when the enemy is Hindered, ModParser.lua:2962-2963). CanWither only lets the Withered stack value be computed (CalcPerform.lua:2756-2763, 6% per stack); the stack COUNT is the config multiplierWitheredStackCount (ConfigOptions.lua:1839, ifFlag CanWither). FR's box is hand-set to 9 (ChaosDamageTakenInc 54 on the enemy), and removing Withering Presence leaves that 54 unchanged, so on FR the notable's Wither is worth 0 in PoB and the DPS delta is only the 15% more chaos damage (ChaosMore 1.15 -> 1). The hinder clause needs conditionEnemyHindered (ConfigOptions.lua:1854, default off); FR has no Hindered source and TotalDot is 0, so it is dead on FR.
- Frigid Wake: 4 of 5 lines parse (ModCache.lua:14562 ChillImmune; 14566 FreezeImmune; 5457 ColdDamage MORE 15; 18328 EnemyModifier Damage INC -10 when the enemy is Chilled, ModParser.lua:2964); "Every 4 seconds, 50% chance to Freeze nearby Non-Frozen Enemies for 0.4 seconds" is unparsed (ModCache.lua:15467 nil), so no freeze is applied. The chilled clause fires when the enemy is Chilled by config conditionEnemyChilled (ConfigOptions.lua:1899, default off) or by a guaranteed-chill skill (CalcPerform.lua:1429-1431; FR: Arctic Armour, probe B_probe.json). The player's immunities appear as ChillImmune / FreezeImmune flags (CalcPerform.lua:872).
- Profane Bloom: the line parses (ModCache.lua:14902: ExplodeMod LIST {Chaos, amount 25, chance 0.5} plus CanExplode FLAG, conditioned on the enemy being Cursed, ModParser.lua:2116). CalcSetup.lua:1507-1545 turns each CanExplode node into a hidden skill group 'On Kill Monster Explosion' (skillId EnemyExplode, Data/Skills/other.lua:6187), whose damage is 25% of data.monsterLifeTable[enemyLevel] (CalcOffence.lua:1916-1928) scaled by the build's chaos modifiers; it has Speed 0 and no includeInFullDPS, so it never shows in TotalDPS or FullDPS unless you select the group as main skill. Measured by selecting it (B_explode.json): ChaosMin/Max 4,317 (None) / 4,066 (Pinnacle) base, average hit 158,462 / 125,424, ExplodeChance 50%. The monster life is the level table, not the boss's life. Conditions: needs the enemy Cursed (automatic with an enabled curse, ConfigOptions.lua:1806).
- Unholy Authority: both lines parse (ModCache.lua:22763 EnemyCurseLimit BASE 1; 23036 CursesIgnoreHexproof FLAG). The flag is consumed at CalcPerform.lua:2662 and 3178 only when the enemy modDB carries Hexproof; PoB has no Hexproof config (no match in ConfigOptions.lua), so it is zero in every PoB run, see [[Curse application and effect]].
- Unhallowed Rite: the line parses to flag Condition:CanGainSpiritInfusion (ModCache.lua:16170); CalcPerform.lua:741-748 and 1017 give SpiritInfusionsMax 10 and, per stack, 15% faster ES recharge start, 5% more damage and 10% more cost to Spell + Channel skills; the stack count is the config overrideSpiritInfusion (ConfigOptions.lua:973, default unset = 0). Wiki rev 1702957 (3.29.0) states the same 15% / 5% / 10%, max 10, lose one every 0.5 s without a gain in 4 s; the Occultist class page (rev 1696954) still shows an older version 3.29.0 row (30% / 10% / 20%, max 5) - same totals at max, stale text. See [[Channelling]] and [[Cast while Channelling]].

On-hit and when-hit sources of this ascendancy (rule from task 027): none of the eight is a hit effect with a hit rate. Withering Presence applies Withered every second for 15 seconds (periodic, one stack per second to a cap of 15; a pack that dies in 5 s carries at most 5 stacks = 30% increased chaos damage taken at 6 per stack, a boss near you reaches 15 stacks = 90% after 15 s); PoB's config box is multiplierWitheredStackCount, ticked at 9 on FR (hand-set, not derived). Frigid Wake is periodic: one 50% roll every 4 s for 0.4 s, expected freeze share 0.5 x 0.4 / 4 = 5% of the time before duration modifiers (the wiki says freeze duration modifiers apply since 3.22.0); PoB has no box and no parse. Profane Bloom is a 50% roll per kill of a cursed enemy, so its uptime is per kill, not per hit.
