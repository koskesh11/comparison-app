A cluster jewel in an allocated large (outer) socket adds a small ring of passives to the tree: small passives with one repeated stat (the enchant), up to 1/2/3 notables from the pool of that enchant, and 0-2 sockets that hold smaller cluster jewels or normal jewels. PoB builds the ring as a subgraph with synthetic node ids; the nodes cost passive points like any other and are allocated by hand. The ring is only as good as what is allocated in it: removing or re-socketing a jewel drops every allocation inside the ring and in the sockets nested in it, and re-socketing does not give them back.
Generator rule: after ANY cluster jewel change (socket, remove, swap, re-socket) the ring allocation is wiped (PassiveSpec.lua:1872, measured: allocated nodes 117 stay 117 after re-socketing) and every ring node has to be allocated again, nested sockets first.
Nested sockets hide dependencies: in the EK sample the Forbidden Flame and Forbidden Flesh jewels sit in the medium cluster sockets, so removing a medium cluster also breaks that pair. Of the -55.5% FullDPS for one medium, -49.7% is the pair (Like Clockwork), see [[Forbidden Flame and Flesh]] (id forbidden-jewels); the medium's own Repeater + Aerodynamics are worth -11.5%.
Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#Increased and reduced]] - the small passives and notables add increased/more/speed mods like any tree node (so the step depends on the notable); the nested-jewel dependency acts earlier, at allocation, because an unallocated socket gives its jewel nothing (CalcSetup.lua:846-848)
