The Champion ascendancy (tree group 427, start node 24984) is the Duelist's fortification, banner, impale and intimidate ascendancy: seven notables on its tree in 3.29, plus the hidden Fatal Flourish 42469, which carries ascendancyName Champion in tree.lua but has no links and is reachable only with matching Forbidden Flesh and Forbidden Flame jewels (wiki Fatal Flourish rev 1418802, 3.18.0). The wiki class page lists only seven older notables and omits Worthy Causes and Unstoppable Hero (3.26.0/3.27.0). No sample build is a Champion: nothing here was measured in a headless run, so there is no measured table; every figure is the tree text, the wiki quote or PoB source. Mechanics that have their own note are linked, not repeated: [[Fortify]] (Fortitude, Unstoppable Hero), [[Impale]] (Master of Metal), [[Intimidate and Unnerve]] (First to Strike), [[Aura effect and aura count]] (Inspirational), [[Armour and physical max hit]], [[Recovery]].

Pipeline: [[Receiving damage#Damage taken modifiers]] - Fortitude's permanent maximum Fortification (1% less damage taken per stack), Unstoppable Hero's armour and evasion; [[Damage calculation#Damage taken]] - the enemy debuffs (Intimidate from First to Strike, Worthy Foe's 15% increased damage taken); [[Damage calculation#Ailments from hits]] and [[Damage calculation#Flat damage]] - Master of Metal's extra impale hit and added physical per impale; [[Damage calculation#Increased and reduced]] - Inspirational's aura effect and attack speed per Fortification; [[Damage calculation#More and less]] - Fatal Flourish's 60% more final repeat; [[Receiving damage#Recovery]] - Inspirational's regeneration and First to Strike's life on Adrenaline.

Graph and point cost (tree.lua `in`/`out` lists, group 427; the cap is 8 ascendancy points, Modules/Build.lua:902). Notables with a second one behind them are strict chains. Fatal Flourish 42469 (tree.lua:90055, group 63033) has empty `in` and `out`, so it costs no path here and is not a pick on this tree.

Small nodes (tree.lua stats): 6982 15% increased Evasion Rating and Armour plus 20% increased Taunt Duration; 41433 the same 15% plus 10% chance to Impale Enemies on Hit with Attacks (the notable itself no longer gives impale chance since 3.13.0); 25111 and 61478 the 15% plus 5% increased effect of Non-Curse Auras from your Skills; 60508 and 35185 the 15% plus 10% increased Damage with Attack Skills while Fortified; 43725 10% increased Attack Damage and 15% increased Evasion Rating and Armour.

Not measured: no sample. The PoB-only arithmetic that follows is from source, not run. Master of Metal: enemy ImpaleStacks defaults to the maximum (CalcPerform.lua:3385-3392: maximum = ImpaleStacksMax x (1 + ImpaleAdditionalDurationChance / 100), base 5 at CalcSetup.lua:49 plus the node's 1 = 6), and the node's 6 to 12 added Physical Damage is per Impale on the enemy, so PoB shows 6 x 6 = 36 to 12 x 6 = 72 added Physical by default even for a build that never impales; the +1000 Armour needs conditionImpaledRecently. Unstoppable Hero is 0 unless maximum Fortification is above 20: FortificationStacksOver20 = min(max(0, stacks - 20), maxStacks - 20) (CalcPerform.lua:670); the evidence found three '+1 to maximum Fortification' small passives in the 3.29 tree (tree.lua:32579, 32596, 32613), which would give at most 3 stacks above 20 = +9% more Armour and Evasion and +9% increased Attack Speed (3 x 3%); no item source was found in Data/Uniques or Data/ModItem.lua (other data files not checked: unverified).

On-hit and when-hit sources of this ascendancy (rule from task 027): (1) Worthy Foe 'Taunt on Hit' - every hit, duration 3 s (wiki Taunt rev 1702419), PoB cannot model the taunt itself; its two damage lines apply only with conditionEnemyTaunted (ConfigOptions.lua:1866, unticked by default). (2) First to Strike 'Your Hits permanently Intimidate Enemies that are on Full Life' - not a chance, permanent once applied; PoB's box conditionChampionIntimidate is ticked by default (ConfigOptions.lua:1486, defaultState true) and ignores the Full Life requirement. DECIDED (user, 2026-10-26): accept PoB's always-on Intimidate - the line reads 'Your Hits permanently Intimidate Enemies that are on Full Life', the first hit lands while the enemy is on Full Life, so the Intimidate is applied then and stays. (3) First to Strike Adrenaline 'when you reach Low Life', 20 seconds once per low-life event; PoB drops the line, the buff exists only through buffAdrenaline (ConfigOptions.lua:1049, unticked). (4) Master of Metal 'If you've Impaled an Enemy Recently' - PoB box conditionImpaledRecently (ConfigOptions.lua:1648, unticked). With no sample there is no PoB hit rate (Speed, HitChance) to put through the vault 'Chance on hit uptime' model, so these uptimes are NOT VERIFIED as numbers.
