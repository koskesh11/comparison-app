The Assassin ascendancy (tree group 792, start node 58229) is a crit ascendancy with eleven selectable notables in 3.29; this note lists every one with its tree text, its wiki rule where it adds something, how PoB parses it, and what it does in the EK seed (class Shadow, ascendancy Assassin; it allocates Unstable Infusion, Deathmarked, Mystical Infusion and Mistwalker, 8 of 8 ascendancy points). Mechanics that have their own note are linked, not repeated: [[Assassin's Mark]] (Deathmarked), [[Power charges]] (Unstable Infusion; its two charge-gain lines are unparsed there too), [[CoC trigger]], [[Critical strike chance]] and [[Critical strike multiplier]] (bifurcation, Jugular), [[Culling strike]] (the cull row), [[Poison]] (Toxic Delivery, Infused Toxins).

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#Critical strike]] - Mystical Infusion bifurcates the spell crit roll and its 30% less lowers the chance before the roll, Jugular adds to the multiplier; [[Receiving damage#Preventing the hit]] - Mistwalker's Elusive avoids hits and removes extra crit damage taken, Opportunistic and Shadowed Blood sit in damage taken and recovery.

Why EK takes each:
- Mystical Infusion feeds the spell: the damage comes from Ethereal Knives (a spell, CoC-triggered by the Cyclone crit), the crit chance is 100 before the roll, so both rolls always succeed and CritBifurcates is 2.0: the extra damage is counted twice (CritMultiplier 9.24 with, 5.12 without, at None). The 30% less spell crit chance costs nothing while the Mark holds the chance at 100: without Deathmarked the pre-roll chance is 91.39 and CritBifurcates drops to 1.84.
- Deathmarked feeds the Mark: 10% increased Mark effect per maximum power charge (7 charges = +70, [[Assassin's Mark]]) and the Mark's added crit chance and multiplier; removing it also drops LifeLeechInstantProportion 0.21 to 0 (3% x 7).
- Mistwalker feeds defence: Condition:CanBeElusive is set only by it (it is false after removal), then ticked buffElusive gives AvoidAllDamageFromHitsChance 21 (15 x ElusiveEffectMod 140%) and MovementSpeedMod 1.561 to 1.267, and CritExtraDamageReduction 100 to 0. PoB's TotalEHP takes the avoid chance and the crit reduction.
- Unstable Infusion is the gate to both crit branches and gives +1 maximum power charge (PowerChargesMax 7 to 6, FrenzyChargesMax follows). Removing only node 4242 and leaving its branch (direct, not through DeallocNode): FullDPS -11.6% / -11.5%, CombinedDPS -9.7% / -9.7%, TotalEHP -1.3% / -1.2%: that part is the extra charge (Mark effect, charge-scaled mods, mana regeneration 209.4 to 203.5). The rest of the -68% comes from losing the two notables and two small nodes behind it (not additive).
