The Chieftain ascendancy (tree group of start node 24704) is the Marauder's fire, warcry and resistance-conversion ascendancy: seven notables in 3.29, five of them one small node off the start (Hinekora, Ngamahu, Ramako, Sione, Tasalio) and two behind another notable's path (Valako behind Tasalio, Tukohama behind Hinekora or Sione). This note lists every notable with its tree text, its wiki rule where it adds something, how PoB parses it (live parse through PoB's own tree pipeline: all seven grade full) and what it does in the two sample builds. Mechanics that have their own note are linked, not repeated: [[Warcries]] and [[Call to Arms]] (Sione), [[Radius jewels]] and [[Cluster jewels]] (Ngamahu), [[Ignite]], [[Damage over time multiplier]], [[Exposure]] and [[Curse application and effect]] (Ramako), [[Totems]] (Hinekora), [[Max resistances]], [[Max res equalise]], [[Defences]] and [[Elemental ailment immunity]] (Tasalio, Valako), [[CoC trigger]] (sample S1). The Scion's Ascendant tree can reach a reduced Chieftain option, see [[Ascendant ascendancy]].

Pipeline: [[Receiving damage#Resistances]] and [[Defences#Resistance caps]] - Tasalio's fire-to-cold/lightning resistance conversion and Valako's maximum-resistance conversion; [[Receiving damage#Immunity and avoidance]] - Tasalio's Unaffected by Ignite; [[Damage calculation#Resistance, exposure and penetration]] and [[Damage calculation#Damage over time]] - Ramako's enemy fire resistance override against damage over time; [[Damage calculation#Increased and reduced]] - Sione's 30% increased warcry buff effect and Ngamahu's transform of increases; [[Damage calculation#More and less]] - Tukohama's Fist of War support; Hinekora's kill explosion is a separate hit outside the main skill's pipeline (see its paragraph).

Small nodes (tree.lua stats, each also Regenerate 0.5% of Life per second): 9971, 14996, 10238 10% increased Fire Damage; 47486 and 982 10% increased Melee Damage; 5643 and 42659 +15% to Fire Resistance; 6028 15% increased Warcry Duration. Node 47486 has an empty `in` list and links Sione and Tukohama only. All seven notables cost 14 points; the cap is 8, so a Chieftain drops at least three of them.

Hinekora, Death's Fury 1731 (tree.lua:16272-16290, name 16274): "Enemies you or your Totems Kill have 10% chance to Explode, dealing 250% of their maximum Life as Fire Damage". Live parse: ExplodeMod {Fire, 250, chance 0.1} and CanExplode, which PoB turns into a separate socket group "On Kill Monster Explosion" (Modules/CalcSetup.lua:1507-1540). Text change 3_27 to 3_29: the sample S1 was saved on 3_27 with "5% chance ... 500%"; PoB's in-memory conversion gave "10% ... 250%" (wiki 3.28.0 row), the same expected value per kill (5% x 500 = 10% x 250 = 25% of maximum Life). PoB does not count the explosion hit: the explosion skill (EnemyExplode) has castTime 0 and showAverage (Data/Skills/other.lua:6207-6224), so as a main skill it shows TotalDPS 0 and Speed 0, and PoB has no kill rate. The group is also not in FullDPS unless its "Include in Full DPS" box is ticked, and then only its ignite part reaches FullDPS. Wiki: low chance, very high damage, a larger radius than similar explosions; the wiki says an explosion caused by a totem does not have the player's offensive stats (a video reference on the wiki, unverified).

Ngamahu, Flame's Advance 50692 (tree.lua:16251-16271, name 16253, stats 16258-16259): "Non-Unique Jewels cause Increases and Reductions to other Damage Types in a Large Radius to be Transformed to apply to Fire Damage" and "Non-Unique Jewels cause Small and Notable Passive Skills in a Large Radius to also grant +4 to Strength". Live parse: two ExtraJewelFunc mods that act only for non-unique jewels in tree sockets (Modules/CalcSetup.lua:905, 1429). Wiki: the transform covers increased and reduced of Physical, Cold, Lightning and Chaos damage and does not change increased Spell Damage or the damage over time multiplier; overlapping jewels stack additively; non-unique cluster jewels count as jewels for nodes in range. It transforms increases, it does not convert damage (see [[Conversion and gain as extra]]).

Ramako, Sun's Light 61355 (tree.lua:16081-16097, name 16083): "Nearby Enemy Monsters' Fire Resistance against Damage over Time is -20% while you are Stationary". Live parse: EnemyModifier FireResist OVERRIDE -20, flag Dot, condition player Stationary (b1d-pca-parse.md:1085). The gate is the config count conditionStationary (Modules/ConfigOptions.lua:158-168; any value above 0 gives the same result, 1 versus 5 measured). Wiki Ramako rev 1491500: "it overrides all other modifiers to enemy fire resistance (e.g. exposure, curses) against damage over time. Hit damage is unaffected." An override sets the resistance to -20; it RAISES it for an enemy that already sits below -20, so it can lower the ignite (measured below). Ruthless: 0% instead of -20%.

Sione, Sun's Roar 31667 (tree.lua:16229-16250, name 16231, stats 16236-16237, reminder 16240): "30% increased Warcry Buff Effect" and "Warcries have infinite Power". Live parse: BuffEffect INC 30 (Warcry keyword) and WarcryInfinitePower, which sets WarcryPower to 999999 (Modules/CalcPerform.lua:1344-1345). So the config box multiplierWarcryPower (Modules/ConfigOptions.lua:743-744) has no effect with Sione allocated. Wiki: the buff effect does not affect exerted attacks. In the game the real warcry power comes from the pack (1 per normal monster, 2 magic, 10 rare, 20 unique, reminder text tree.lua:16240); PoB assumes 0 power on a map pack and the boss preset power (20 for Pinnacle, ConfigOptions.lua:2158). That is why Sione's value in PoB at None is mostly PoB's zero warcry power on a pack (measured below).

Tasalio, Cleansing Water 48480 (tree.lua:16020-16042, name 16022, stats 16027-16028, reminder 16031): "Modifiers to Fire Resistance also apply to Cold and Lightning Resistances at 50% of their Value" and "Unaffected by Ignite". Live parse: FireResConvertToCold 50 and FireResConvertToLightning 50 (Modules/CalcDefence.lua:552), SelfIgniteEffect MORE -100 (CalcDefence.lua:1669). Wiki: the debuff is applied and shown but has no effect, which is what PoB models (consistent). The 50% applies to fire resistance modifiers, not to the overcap line.

Tukohama, War's Herald 53095 (tree.lua:16043-16061, name 16045, stats 16050-16051): "Skills from Equipped Body Armour are Supported by Level 30 Ancestral Call" and "... Level 20 Fist of War". Live parse: two ExtraSupport mods tagged SocketedIn Body Armour (Modules/CalcSetup.lua:1654). Wiki: the two supports have mutually exclusive tags, so a 7-link slam or strike skill or two 6-links. Class page 3.25.0: the notable needs Sione or Hinekora (tree.lua `in` = 47486 and 982).

Valako, Storm's Embrace 32249 (tree.lua:16098-16114, name 16100, stats 16105): "Modifiers to Maximum Fire Resistance also apply to Maximum Cold and Lightning Resistances". Live parse: FireMaxResConvertToCold 100 and FireMaxResConvertToLightning 100 (Modules/CalcDefence.lua:532). Wiki: it does not interact with modifiers to maximum cold or lightning resistance (those still apply). See [[Max res equalise]].

S2 Sione is gated by the warcry power the config assumes. With Sione on, the result is the same at warcry power 10, 20, 30, 999 and unset (2,872,017.0, None), because Sione overrides the power. With Sione off, FullDPS (None / Pinnacle) is: power unset 1,897,054.1 / 1,972,054.3 (the unset default is 0 power for None and the 20 of the Pinnacle preset), power 10 2,246,130.8 / 1,700,227.4, power 20 2,595,207.5 / 1,972,054.3, power 30 and 999 2,768,778.0 / 2,107,214.0 (same, so the cap is reached at 30 power in this build's warcry set-up). Sione's two halves: the 30% warcry buff effect alone is +3.73% (None) / +3.82% (Pinnacle) once power is at least 30; the infinite Power alone is +45.9% at the None default (power unset) and +6.9% at the Pinnacle default (power 20), and 0% once power is already at least 30; both together +51.4% / +10.9% over Sione-off at the defaults, hence the -33.95% / -9.85% above. So at None, Sione's S2 value is mostly PoB's zero warcry power on a pack.

Ramako, not allocated in either sample, allocated hypothetically (small node 10238 plus Ramako, restore equal to base): S2 with conditionStationary 0 gives FullDPS +1.13% (None) / +1.12% (Pinnacle), which is the small node's +10% Fire Damage. With conditionStationary 1 the Best Ignite DPS part falls from 91,833.8 to 63,699.8 (-30.6%) at None and from 65,292.3 to 63,699.8 (-2.4%) at Pinnacle; FullDPS ends +0.15% over the S2 base at None. A control run (stationary 1 without Ramako) changed nothing. S2's enemy fire resistance is -73 (None) / -23 (Pinnacle) in PoB's enemy modifier list (composition not itemised; the build has a Flammability group), and Ramako's override sets it to -20, which is HIGHER, so Ramako lowers the ignite. This is the TAA-C surprise: it is not a PoB artefact, the wiki says the same (override of all other fire resistance modifiers against damage over time, rev 1491500). In S1, Ramako plus stationary gives +1.22% FullDPS, all from the small node (Firestorm of Meteors is a hit, no ignite part). It answers the open question in [[Ignite]] ("is the enemy fire resistance applied to the ignite in PoB?"): yes, the override moved Best Ignite DPS.

Other notables allocated hypothetically (2 nodes each, restored): S1 Sione +0.00% (no warcry in the build), Tukohama +0.01% (Cyclone +1.78%, Firestorm of Meteors unchanged; the notable's two supports were not separated from the small node's melee damage); S2 Hinekora +1.13% (the small node only, the explosion adds nothing), Ngamahu +2.15% None and Pinnacle (FullDPS 2,933,769.5; Strength 222 to 310 = 22 nodes x 4 with 6 rare cluster jewels, TotalEHP +1.5%), Tukohama +0.00% (S2's body armour holds warcries, which Ancestral Call and Fist of War do not support). Whether a gem in the body armour can use the two supports was not verified.

On-hit and when-hit sources of this ascendancy (rule from task 027): the only chance line on the Chieftain tree is Hinekora's, and it is on kill (10% chance per kill of an enemy you or your totems kill), not on hit; no Chieftain notable carries a chance-on-hit line (the "Cover in Ash on Hit" line sits only in the Ascendant option, see [[Ascendant ascendancy]]). PoB has no kill rate, so the explosion uptime per scenario cannot be computed with the vault 'Chance on hit uptime' model and is NOT VERIFIED as a number: in a map it is 0.1 times the kill rate (explosions per second); against a pinnacle boss only the adds' kills count, since the boss's own kill ends the fight. The sample's hit-rate output fields (Speed, HitChance) were not read, so no per-scenario p is given. The other six notables are static or condition-gated, not chance-based: Tasalio, Valako, Ngamahu and Tukohama are always on, Ramako only while Stationary (PoB's count is binary, no uptime fraction), Sione only while the warcry buff is up (PoB assumes it is).
