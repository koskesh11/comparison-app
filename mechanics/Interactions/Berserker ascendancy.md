The Berserker ascendancy (tree start node 29294) is the Marauder's Rage, attack-speed and warcry ascendancy: seven notables on its tree in 3.29 plus the hidden Indomitable Resolve 52435, an orphan node with no links that is granted only through matching Forbidden Flame and Forbidden Flesh jewels ([[Forbidden Flame and Flesh]]). This note lists every notable with its tree text, its wiki rule, how PoB parses it (live parse through PoB's own tree pipeline), what PoB does not count, and what the two allocated notables do in the one sample build, 3.26/VFoS Berserker.xml (Marauder/Berserker, level 67, spec 4 'Level 38-67 {4}', 94 allocated nodes; it allocates Crave the Slaughter and Rite of Ruin, so 4 ascendancy points). Live-parse grades: 6 full (Aspect of Carnage, Crave the Slaughter, Gore Dancer, Indomitable Resolve, Rite of Ruin, War Bringer), 1 partial (Defy Pain, 1 of 4 lines applied), 1 none (Ancestral Fury). The tree text of all eight notables, and of every other node of the ascendancy, is identical in tree.lua 3_26, 3_27, 3_28 and 3_29. Mechanics that have their own note are linked, not repeated: [[Warcries]] and [[Call to Arms]] (War Bringer, the exert model behind Crave's measured number), [[Leech]] and [[Recovery]] (Gore Dancer, Rite of Ruin's life drain), [[Stun]], [[Damage calculation]], [[Receiving damage]]. Rage and Defiance have no vault note yet; they are explained here in plain text (candidate mechanic ids: rage, defiance).

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#More and less]] - Aspect of Carnage's 40% more Damage and Rage's inherent more Attack Damage; [[Damage calculation#Hit rate]] - Crave's attack speed per Rage and War Bringer's doubled exerts; [[Receiving damage#Damage taken modifiers]] - Aspect of Carnage's 10% increased damage taken, Indomitable Resolve's 25% less damage taken; [[Receiving damage#Recovery]] - Rite of Ruin's life drain, Gore Dancer's instant leech, Defy Pain's recovery before a hit.

Small nodes (tree.lua stats): 50024 20% increased Warcry Speed and 10% increased Physical Damage; 63673 5% increased Attack Speed and 10% increased Physical Damage; 48904 30% increased total Recovery per second from Life Leech and 10% increased Physical Damage; 63583 and 42861 'Gain 1 Rage on Attack Hit' and 10% increased Physical Damage each (both allocated in the sample); 8592 +0.2 metres to Melee Strike Range and 10% increased Physical Damage; 5865 15% increased Armour and 10% increased Physical Damage. Crave the Slaughter 24528 (tree.lua:14510, name line 14512, stats 14517-14518, reminders 14521-14522): "Every Rage also grants 1% increased Attack Speed", "Inherent Rage Loss starts 2 seconds later". Wiki rev 1479898: the attack speed per Rage is part of Rage and scales with Rage effect (PoB: keyed to Multiplier:RageEffect, so Rite of Ruin's 50% increased Rage Effect feeds it; 3.25.0 replaced the old +10 maximum Rage line with these two). Live parse: Speed INC 1 (attacks) per RageEffect (ModCache.lua:15486), InherentRageLossDelay BASE 2 (CalcPerform.lua:932); grade config (user grading rule 2026-10-26: parsed fully, 0% until the box is set), gate Rage (below). PoB does not count Rage gain or Rage decay (below).

Rite of Ruin 57560 (tree.lua:14712, name line 14714, stats 14719-14720, reminder 14723): "Lose 0.1% of Life per second per Rage while you are not losing Rage", "50% increased Rage Effect". Wiki rev 1481091: the effect raises anything that scales with Rage, including its own life loss; the loss is not damage and cannot be mitigated; 3.25.0 replaced the old 'inherent effects tripled' and 'Cannot be Stunned while 25 Rage' lines (the same stun line is now only on the Ascendant's multiple-choice option named 'Berserker', node 4194, see [[Stun]]). Live parse: LifeDegen BASE 1 as 0.1% of Life per RageEffect (ModCache.lua:17452) and RageEffect INC 50 (ModCache.lua:10278); grade config (user grading rule 2026-10-26: parsed fully, 0% until the box is set), gate Rage. PoB drops the 'while you are not losing Rage' condition, so the life drain is applied always (b1d-pca-parse.md:1028), not only while Rage is held steady.

War Bringer 32251 (tree.lua:14695, name line 14697, stats 14702): "Warcries Exert twice as many Attacks". Wiki rev 1463235 (3.25.0 replaced the old rage and sacrifice lines). Live parse: ExtraExertedAttacks MORE 100 (ModCache.lua:22423; CalcPerform.lua:2370-2371); grade full, no gate beyond having warcries. Not allocated in the sample, so not measured; PoB's exert share is ExertedAttackUptimeRatio (59.04% in the sample, see measured) and the doubled count can reach the 100% ceiling, so the gain has diminishing returns. PoB does not count warcry cooldown or ammo state beyond its average ratio. See [[Warcries]], [[Call to Arms]].

Aspect of Carnage 59920 (tree.lua:14533, name line 14535, stats 14540-14541): "10% increased Damage taken", "40% more Damage". Wiki rev 1418699 (2.2.0 added, 3.22.0 Ruthless 25% more). Live parse: DamageTaken INC 10, Damage MORE 40; grade full, no gate. Always on; not allocated in the sample, not measured. Gore Dancer sits behind it.

Gore Dancer 29630 (tree.lua:14572, name line 14574, stats 14579): "30% of Life Leech is Instant". Wiki rev 1546857 (3.26.0 replaced Blitz). Live parse: InstantLifeLeech BASE 30 (ModCache.lua:8416; CalcOffence.lua:3730); grade full. Needs a leech source; alone it is 0. A share of the leech becomes instant, still capped by the per-instance maximum (see [[Leech]], [[Recovery]]). Not allocated in the sample, not measured.

Defy Pain 9271 (tree.lua:14733, name line 14735, stats 14740-14742): "Gain Defiance for 10 seconds on losing Life to an Enemy Hit, no more than once every 0.3 seconds", "Lose all Defiance when you reach 10 Defiance", "Gain 3% of Missing Unreserved Life before being Hit by an Enemy Per Defiance". Wiki rev 1642261 (3.26.0 reworked into this text): the recovery happens before the hit's damage is calculated (example on the page: 1/5000 life with 8 stacks (24%) recovers 1199 life), same as Defiance of Destiny, stacks last 10 s. Rage gate (the value is 0 without a hand-set box): PoB shows the Rage config box 'Rage:' as multiplierRage (ConfigOptions.lua:1073, visible with the flag Condition:CanGainRage, which five tree nodes set here: 23038 Slaughter, 34590 and 48199 Rage on Hit, and the two small nodes 42861 and 63583). The XML has 49, capped at the maximum Rage of 47 (30 base + 10 Battle Trance 24716 + 7 Rage Mastery 9471); RageEffect 70 with Rite of Ruin, inherent Rage loss delay 4 s (2 base + 2 Crave) and loss 12 per second (10 x 1.2 from Rage Mastery). With multiplierRage unset (default 0): Rage 0, RageEffect 0, and both Rage notables are worth 0.00% for Volcanic Fissure of Snaking and for the variant FullDPS (None and Pinnacle); the main skill's TotalDPS falls to 99,178.6 (None) and 56,076.2 (Pinnacle), about 45% below the saved values. The XML ticks the box, so the user already supplies the Rage. DECIDED (user, 2026-10-26): with a reliable Rage source the generator sets multiplierRage to half of maximum Rage, rounded down (here floor(47 / 2) = 23; not run headless); see [[Rage]]. Crave and Rite are graded config (parsed fully, 0% until the box is set).

What PoB does not count for these two notables: (1) Rage gain. Only the hand-set stack count exists. In the game each Rage source gives 'Gain 1 Rage on Attack Hit' with one shared 0.5 s cooldown (wiki Rage rev 1700756; tree.lua reminder 14641 'Only one Hit every 0.5 seconds can cause you to gain Rage'), so at most 2 gaining hits per second; whether the per-source amounts add up inside that cooldown is unverified. (2) Rage loss. Rage decays at 12 per second after 4 s without a hit or Rage gain in the sample; a gap longer than that (an untargetable boss phase, no monsters) drops 47 Rage in about 4 s, while PoB keeps 47 permanently. (3) Rite of Ruin's life drain is applied always, although the node says only while Rage is not being lost; the drain is not damage and cannot be mitigated, and TotalEHP ignores it. (4) Whether the fissure of Volcanic Fissure of Snaking counts as an Attack Hit for Rage on Hit is not verified.

On-hit and when-hit sources of this ascendancy (rule from task 027): (1) Rage on Attack Hit (the small nodes 42861 and 63583, plus other tree nodes): a hit-based gain, not a chance; PoB output fields on the sample are Speed 1.9316 attacks per second and HitChance 100 (evidence b5a-taa-jb.md section 3), one shared 0.5 s cooldown, so at most 2 gaining hits per second; Rage lasts while hits continue and decays after 4 s; value when up: the Crave and Rite lines measured above; PoB's box is multiplierRage and is ticked (49) in the sample. With the vault 'Chance on hit uptime' model p = 1 for an Attack Hit and m = 1 gaining hit per 0.5 s, so the stack count at the time of a pack is high while the character keeps hitting; the time to build 47 Rage depends on the per-hit amounts of the five sources, which the evidence does not give: NOT VERIFIED as a number. (2) Defy Pain's Defiance on losing Life to an Enemy Hit: at most once per 0.3 s, 10 s duration, reset at 10 stacks; PoB box multiplierDefiance, default 0 (not ticked); with the vault assumption E = 1 boss hit per second (Pantheon and bandits.md:116) a 10-hit cycle runs 0 to 9 stacks before each hit, 4.5 on average, so 13.5% of missing life per hit; this is arithmetic from the node text, not run in PoB. (3) Crave and Rite have no chance-on-hit line of their own.
