Heralds are Spell/Buff skills with the Herald tag. Each reserves a base 25% of mana (auras reserve 50%, 35% or a flat amount), gives a buff while active, and adds a secondary effect when enemies are killed, shattered or shocked. PoB counts the reservation, the buff, the Herald count (for "per Herald affecting you" lines) and the Herald conditions automatically; it does not model how often the kill-triggered secondary effect fires (only a hand-set Herald of Ash overkill number).
What builds can steer: two separate effect pools (global "Effect of Herald Buffs on you" plus per-herald "Herald of X has N% increased Buff Effect" add up in one increased pool, "more" lines multiply), reservation efficiency lines that name Herald skills, and the damage lines that need a Herald active. Herald of Ash, Ice and Thunder deal their damage as buff and burst; Herald of Purity and Agony summon minions (generic lines only here; single-herald numbers belong in per-skill notes).

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#Gain as extra]], [[Damage calculation#Increased and reduced]] - the herald buff enters as extra damage or added flat damage scaled by buff effect, and the Herald count turns on "per Herald" and "while affected by a Herald" increased lines; the reservation side acts in [[Receiving damage#Mana before life]]
