Elemental Equilibrium (EE) is a keystone: a Hit that deals elemental damage removes the enemy's Exposure to the elements it dealt and inflicts Exposure (-25% to that resistance) to the elements it did not deal. It exists since the 3.16.0 rework (the old version swapped resistances). For a build that rotates two or three elements it is a strong exposure source; for a single-element build it only removes the build's own exposure. PoB models it only through config boxes: with the keystone allocated and no box ticked, PoB shows NO change at all (measured below), because PoB reads the enemy's "hit by" state from the config before the main skill's own hit is calculated. The wiki and PoB also disagree on same-element on-hit exposure (Eye of Malice); both readings stay visible. Related: [[Exposure]] (the stacking rules and the first EE conflict entry), [[Chance on hit uptime]], [[Avatar of Fire]] (no synergy), [[Damage calculation]].

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#Resistance, exposure and penetration]] - EE decides which of the three elemental exposures are on the enemy when a hit's resistance is read: it lowers the resistance of the elements NOT dealt by 25 (strongest exposure per element applies, extras like Mastermind of Discord add) and deletes the exposure of the elements dealt, so it acts on the uncapped resistance before penetration.

Reading: the 'hit by Cold' column for Worb is what the game does to a cold-only build on every hit (own cold exposure removed); only the box makes PoB show it. The 'hit by Fire' columns are the real gain of EE: another element in the rotation. Fireball gains +41% only if a cold or lightning hit comes before it (Mastermind of Discord's extra -25 makes the exposure -50). Worb's 'hit by Fire/Lightning' gain is +15.36% / +10.16% because Worb already carries cold exposure -35 (Eye of Malice box -10, Mastermind -25) and EE's -25 + -25 = -50 only adds -15. Worb.xml has no Frost Bomb (grep count 0): its cold exposure comes from Eye of Malice (25% chance) plus the ticked box conditionEnemyColdExposure, and Worb cannot deal non-cold damage (Bitter Frost, tree node 12128), so EE can only lose there.
