Cast when Damage Taken (CwDT) triggers a linked spell each time the damage you take from HITS adds up to a threshold (L14: 1980, L20: 3272, PoB level rows). It counts the damage after all mitigation (block included), only from hits (not damage over time), keeps one counter per supported spell, and does not count while that spell is on cooldown. PoB computes only the threshold (display) and then triggers the spell at its COOLDOWN cap, as if enough damage always arrived: the threshold is never compared with the hit the enemy deals. The trigger rate of a triggered curse or guard skill is therefore an upper bound that depends on how often and how hard the enemy hits (scenario input, default enemy attack time 700 ms). PoB also does not enforce the gem's maximum supported skill level: both seed samples have a triggered spell above it.

Pipeline: [[Receiving damage#Block]] and [[Receiving damage#Guard and Aegis]] - the counter reads the 'final damage taken' value, which the wiki places after block and before damage is applied to guard, energy shield and life; so only damage that survived resistances, armour, suppression and block counts, and a guard skill triggered by it can never save you from the hit that triggered it. Mana for the triggered spell: [[Total skill cost must be sustainable]].
