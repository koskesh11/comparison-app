Some uniques carry lines that read "while affected by <aura>" or "while using Pride". PoB sets one condition per active aura gem (AffectedBy<SkillName>), so these lines are on exactly as long as that aura skill is on and not Generosity-supported; the gem does not need to be reserved. Watcher's Eye rolls 2 or 3 such lines from a pool; for the EK seed (Pride + Clarity) two of its lines hang on the two aura gems, so dropping Pride removes the impale line AND Pride's own enemy debuff. Aul's Uprising and Victario's Charity work on the reservation / area side instead: Aul's sets one aura's reservation to 0 (the aura stays on, its Watcher's Eye lines stay on).
One line is not a plain on/off: Clarity "chance to Recover 10% of Mana when you use a Skill" parses to a wrong Mana BASE with leftover text, so PoB drops the line (red text).

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#Increased and reduced]] - the Watcher's Eye damage lines (Pride physical, Anger fire inc / extra / conversion / penetration, crit multi) enter the normal pools once the condition tag is true; [[Receiving damage#Mana before life]] - Clarity's mana-before-life line
