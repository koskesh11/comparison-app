The Saboteur ascendancy (Shadow, 10 notables and 12 small nodes in 3.29) is the trap, mine, area and trigger ascendancy: Perfect Crime and Like Clockwork act on triggered spells and cooldowns, Bomb Specialist, Calculated Risk and Harness the Void change the damage of hits, Born in the Shadows blinds enemies, Pyromaniac gives ignite and shock immunity, and Chain Reaction, Demolitions Specialist and Shrapnel Specialist serve traps, mines and projectiles. This note lists every notable with its tree text, its wiki rule where it adds something, how PoB parses it (live parse through PoB's own tree pipeline: 4 full, 5 partial, 1 none, grades from the PCA-B parse) and what it does in the two sample builds. Mechanics that have their own note are linked, not repeated: [[CoC trigger]] and [[Trigger rate cap and cooldown]] (Perfect Crime, Like Clockwork), [[Cooldown recovery breakpoints]] (Like Clockwork), [[Lucky critical strikes]] and [[Critical strike chance]] (Calculated Risk), [[Blind]], [[Accuracy and hit chance]] and [[Chance on hit uptime]] (Born in the Shadows), [[Traps and mines]] (Chain Reaction, Demolitions Specialist, Pyromaniac, throwing-speed small nodes), [[Maim and Hinder]] (Demolitions Specialist's hinder), [[Elemental ailment immunity]] (Pyromaniac), [[Returning projectiles]] and [[Projectile hits single target]] (Shrapnel Specialist), [[Conversion and gain as extra]] and [[Forbidden Flame and Flesh]] (Harness the Void), [[Damage calculation]] and [[Receiving damage]]. The Scion's Ascendant tree can reach a reduced Saboteur option, see [[Ascendant ascendancy]]. Candidate mechanic ids with no vault note yet: triggerbots (Perfect Crime), blind-aura (Born in the Shadows), gain-as-extra-averaged (Harness the Void), averaged chance-more (Bomb Specialist).

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#More and less]] - Perfect Crime's 30% less and Bomb Specialist's averaged area-hit more; [[Damage calculation#Hit rate]] - Perfect Crime's doubled trigger rate and Like Clockwork's trigger-rate cap (the rate comes from the trigger source's hit rate, capped by the cooldown); [[Damage calculation#Critical strike]] and [[Damage calculation#Damage roll]] - Calculated Risk's lucky crit chance and unlucky hits; [[Damage calculation#Gain as extra]] - Harness the Void; [[Receiving damage#Damage taken modifiers]] - Born in the Shadows' 15% reduced damage taken from Blinded enemies; [[Receiving damage#Preventing the hit]] - the blinded enemy's lower accuracy; [[Receiving damage#Immunity and avoidance]] - Pyromaniac and Born in the Shadows' immunities.

Small nodes (tree.lua stats, all 12 parse full): Area Damage 62067 and 63135 (8% increased Area of Effect, 10% increased Area Damage); Blind Effect 64842 (8% increased Area of Effect, 15% increased Blind Effect); Mine Throwing Speed 25167 and 47366, Trap Throwing Speed 16212 and 26446, Trap and Mine Throwing Speed 1953 (8% increased Area of Effect plus 5% increased throwing speed); Movement Speed 869 and 41081 (4% increased Movement Speed, 8% increased Area of Effect); Projectile Speed and Damage 64785 and 65085 (8% increased Projectile Speed, 10% increased Projectile Damage). Perfect Crime 28535 (tree.lua:84321-84329, name 84323, stats 84328-84329): "Triggers Level 20 Summon Triggerbots when Allocated" and "30% less Damage with Triggered Spells". Live parse: HaveTriggerBots FLAG (ModCache.lua:22318) and Damage MORE -30 on Spell with a Triggered skill-type tag (ModCache.lua:8390); grade full. PoB adds no skill for the bots; the flag doubles the trigger rate of a triggered Spell main skill, and the doubling comes AFTER the cap: `SkillTriggerRate = 2 * SkillTriggerRate` (CalcTriggers.lua:272 for the CWC handler, :815-819 for the general handler that includes Cast on Critical Strike). So in S2 the triggered spells' rate (16.249) is above the trigger rate cap (10.101). Wiki (Perfect Crime rev 1619149, Summon Triggerbots rev 1649423): two invulnerable Triggerbot minions, every triggered spell triggers twice (once from each bot), the skill cost is paid twice, the second trigger ignores cooldowns but only happens if the first was off cooldown, the 30% less applies to secondary damage and damage over time of triggered spells (not to minions they summon), Chains of Command and Wraithlord despawn the bots, it works with Awakened Cast on Critical Strike. Text change 3_27 to 3_29: 35% to 30% less (wiki 3.28.0). Role: scales. Gate: the triggered skill must have the Spell type, so a triggered attack is not doubled (S2 Lancing Steel of Spraying: 0.00%).

Like Clockwork 51462 (tree.lua:84689-84697, name 84691, stats 84696-84697): "50% increased Cooldown Recovery Rate" and "Nearby Enemies have 10% reduced Cooldown Recovery Rate". Live parse: CooldownRecovery INC 50 (ModCache.lua:10157) and EnemyModifier CooldownRecovery INC -10 (ModCache.lua:18376); grade full. PCA-B found no reader of the enemy modifier in Modules/Calc*.lua (grep CooldownRecovery: skill side only), so the enemy half has no effect in PoB; whether the game's enemy half matters is not verified. Text change: 40% to 50% (wiki 3.28.0; worth 0.00% in both samples, same trigger-rate-cap bracket). The player half moves the Cast on Critical Strike trigger rate cap: removing it drops the cap from 10.101 to 7.576 triggers per second (the 52% cooldown recovery breakpoint of [[Cooldown recovery breakpoints]] and [[CoC trigger]]). Wiki rev 1619150: it delays enemies from using cooldown skills such as the Maven's memory game.

Perfect Crime in S2: its own removal costs -28.4% because it takes away the x2 trigger rate AND the 30% less in one step: Reap's AverageDamage rises from 856,998 to 1,219,239 (+42.3%, 1 / 0.7) while the rate halves, net 0.7 x 2 = 1.4 gone (-29.0% on both triggered spells). Best Ignite DPS and Full DoT DPS are not scaled by the trigger rate in PoB, so they only gain the lost 30% (+42%). The text change 35% to 30% alone is worth +7.9% in S2 (isolation run on the 3_27 tree). Like Clockwork: S1 sits close to the cap (rate 9.935 against cap 10.101), so the lower cap bites hard (-32.4%); S2's rate is above the cap after the Triggerbot doubling, so the same step costs -8.5%. Calculated Risk in S1: removing it raises Bladefall's AverageDamage 269,606 to 289,056 (+7.2%, the main skill) and Lancing Steel of Spraying's AverageDamage 8,755 to 9,707 (+10.87%), yet FullDPS falls 12.1% because Reap loses 17.7%. The numbers behind it: without the lucky crit Lancing's CritChance goes 98.29 to 86.94, Reap's 88.22 to 65.68, and the Cast on Critical Strike SkillTriggerRate 9.935 to 9.321 (-6.2%, the source crits less often); Bladefall's CritChance stays 100. So the node trades unlucky damage rolls on every hit (Lancing +10.87% and Bladefall +7.2% without it) for a higher source crit chance and more crit-triggered casts of Reap; the main-skill reading (Bladefall +7.2% when removed) says remove it, the FullDPS reading (-12.1%) says keep it. This answers the open question in [[CoC trigger]] "does the lucky crit raise the chance in the CoC check": yes, in PoB the source's crit chance feeds the trigger rate. Bomb Specialist: the chain removal (-24.3%) also removes Calculated Risk (-12.1%) and small node 63135 (+10% Area Damage, about -1.2%); Bomb Specialist's own mods are -12.8% (S1) and -13.3% (S2). Why S2's Reap and Bladefall fall 13.58% where 1 / 1.15 gives 13.04% was not traced.
