An attack hit rolls against the enemy's Evasion with your Accuracy; if it fails it is a miss, and accuracy is rolled a second time for the crit. Spells and minion attacks skip the roll. For a crit-trigger build this is a hidden multiplier: Cast On Critical Strike needs a hit AND a crit, so every point of missing hit chance is lost twice (measured below). Both seeds (EK, FR) show 100% in PoB and their dagger carries the crafted mod "Hits can't be Evaded". RESOLVED (user, 2026-10-26: "spells dont have hit chance. they always hit."; wiki Accuracy rev 1612031: "This does not affect critical strikes by spells."): Ethereal Knives and Forbidden Rite of Soul Sacrifice are spells and always hit, 100% by construction and not because of that mod. The crafted "Hits can't be Evaded" matters only for Cyclone, the attack whose hit and crit rolls set the CoC trigger rate that feeds EK and Forbidden Rite. The numbers below are Cyclone's, PoB in-memory removal delta, not a game fact: with the mod removed in the headless run Cyclone shows 46% against a Pinnacle boss and 51% against a map monster (enemyIsBoss None: PoB takes the enemy level from the character level capped at 85, so Evasion 8388), and the CoC procs 21% and 26% (0.46^2, 0.51^2). EK and FR DPS drops only through Cyclone's trigger rate. Resolute Technique also gives 100% but forbids crits, so it cannot be combined with a CoC build.

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#Hit chance]] - the roll happens before damage is dealt and again at the crit, so it multiplies the rate of hits and of crits ([[Damage calculation#Critical strike]]); the trigger rate of [[CoC trigger]] and [[Trigger rate cap and cooldown]] inherits it. Related: [[Blind]] (enemy Evasion x 0.8).
