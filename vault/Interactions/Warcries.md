A warcry gives a buff scaled by POWER (sum of nearby enemies: normal 1, magic 2, rare 10, unique 20, player 5) and makes your next few attacks EXERTED (stronger, per warcry). Six warcries exert in PoB data (Ancestral 8, Infernal 6, Intimidating 2, Rallying 5, Seismic 6, Battlemage's 5 attacks, all cooldown 8 s, cast time 0.8 s); Enduring Cry and General's Cry do not exert for you. PoB DOES compute the exerted-attack uptime: attacks per warcry / attack rate over cooldown plus cast time, summed with stored uses, capped at 100%, and mixes the exerted effect into damage as a time-weighted average (config warcryMode switches to max hit). What it does not do by itself is the power: only boss presets set 20; for packs the power has to be set by hand. Totems, triggered, channelling and retaliation attacks, repeats and proxies are never exerted, and PoB's gem data exert only melee attacks (slam for Seismic Cry).

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#More and less]] - exerted-attack effects are folded into the hit as an extra multiplier next to double damage and fist of war (allMult, CalcOffence.lua:3440-3446), not into the increased pool; Intimidating Cry's exert is a double-damage factor ([[Damage calculation#Double damage]]); the uptime comes from [[Damage calculation#Hit rate]] (attacks per second against the warcry cycle).
