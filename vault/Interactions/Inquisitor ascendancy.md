The Templar's Inquisitor ascendancy has eight notables in 3.29 (tree.lua:16424-16708): Instruments of Justice, Sanctuary, Pious Path, Instruments of Zeal, Instruments of Virtue, Righteous Providence, Inevitable Judgement and Augury of Penitence. The tree graph (script F_graph.py over tree.lua) is a set of short chains from the Inquisitor start node 61871: Virtue is the hub for Zeal and Justice (Virtue 19417 -> minor 27055 -> Zeal 13851; Virtue -> minor 35739 -> Justice 3154), Sanctuary leads to Pious Path (via minor 57222), Righteous Providence leads to Inevitable Judgement (via minor 37486), Augury stands alone behind minor 60769. Deallocating a parent therefore drops its dependants (PassiveSpec:DeallocNode, Classes/PassiveSpec.lua:830, as in the Occultist note). Every minor on the way gives 10% increased Elemental Damage plus one small stat (4% attack and cast speed, 0.5% life regeneration, +10% critical strike multiplier, or +6% all elemental resistances; tree.lua:16405-16646). Not measured: no seed of this class, so every statement below is a source reading, not a headless run. Other notes own the mechanics: [[Consecrated ground]], [[Critical strike chance]], [[Critical strike multiplier]], [[Exposure]], [[Damage calculation]], [[Energy shield recovery keystones]], [[Vaal Pact]]; [[Elemental ailment count]] is fed by none of the eight (no line counts ailment types).

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#Critical strike]] (Justice, Righteous Providence, Inevitable Judgement), [[Damage calculation#Flat damage]] (Battlemage of Virtue), [[Damage calculation#More and less]] (Virtue, Augury less damage dealt), [[Damage calculation#Hit rate]] (Fanaticism cast speed), [[Damage calculation#Resistance, exposure and penetration]] (Inevitable Judgement), [[Damage calculation#Damage taken]] (Sanctuary, Augury), [[Receiving damage#Recovery]] (Pious Path).

How PoB applies each notable (ModCache.lua line; "full" only when every line yields mods):
- Instruments of Justice 3154 (feeds [[Critical strike chance]]): the line parses to flag BaseCritFromMainHand with mod flag 2 = spells (ModCache.lua:14352); CalcOffence.lua:3085-3089 replaces the skill's base crit with actor.weaponData1.CritChance, so later increased and more crit mods apply to the weapon's base, as the wiki says (rev 1702956: other crit overrides do not apply, unarmed gives 0, secondary spell damage such as Herald explosions is unaffected). Wand base 8.5 is the example already in the Critical strike chance note. Needs Virtue allocated first (path above).
- Sanctuary 39790 (feeds [[Consecrated ground]] and the enemy side of [[Damage calculation#Damage taken]]): both lines parse. "Consecrated Ground you create applies 15% increased Damage taken to Enemies" is an EnemyModifier DamageTakenConsecratedGround INC 15 under enemy Condition OnConsecratedGround (ModCache.lua:14765); CalcPerform.lua:3662-3665 adds floor(15 x (1 + ConsecratedGroundEffect)) to the enemy DamageTaken INC. "You have Consecrated Ground around you while stationary" sets the player's Condition:OnConsecratedGround under Condition Stationary (ModCache.lua:22886), which gives 5% life regeneration and -50% curse effect on you (CalcPerform.lua:804-808, 3145-3148). The two sides have separate gates: the player's ground needs conditionStationary above 0 (ConfigOptions.lua:158-169) and the enemy's needs the enemy-side box conditionEnemyOnConsecratedGround (ConfigOptions.lua:2012-2013); grep of Modules/ shows nothing that copies the player's flag to the enemy, so with the node but without the enemy box PoB shows no damage-taken effect (the same trap as in the Consecrated ground note). No wiki page exists for this notable (the page Sanctuary is a map; Sanctuary (passive skill) is a shield notable, rev 1627613); only the class page row (rev 1696956) lists it.
- Pious Path 32816 (feeds [[Consecrated ground]], [[Receiving damage#Recovery]]): partial. Line 1 parses to ExtraAura flag LifeRegenerationRecoversEnergyShield under OnConsecratedGround (ModCache.lua:14769, ModParser.lua:2921), but grep of the PoB Lua (Modules, Classes, Data, excluding ModCache) finds no reader of that flag, so it changes no output. Line 2 "Effects of Consecrated Ground you create Linger for 4 seconds" is unparsed (ModCache.lua:15226 nil), so the ground never lingers in PoB. Wiki (rev 1549086): the ES regeneration equals your life regeneration, so Zealot's Oath (which moves life regeneration to ES) leaves Pious Path with nothing extra and Shavronne's Revelation blocks it; the linger includes extra ground effects such as Bottled Faith's damage taken and stacks additively with Rational Doctrine. See [[Energy shield recovery keystones]] and [[Vaal Pact]] for the Zealot's Oath and Vaal Pact conflicts.
- Instruments of Zeal 13851 (feeds [[Damage calculation#Hit rate]] through cast speed): partial, 1 of 4 lines yields mods. "Gain Fanaticism for 5 seconds on reaching Maximum Fanatic Charges" is flag Condition:CanGainFanaticism (ModCache.lua:16141, ModParser.lua:2914-2916). The other three are unparsed: "+3 to Maximum Fanatic Charges" (ModCache.lua:1651, empty mods), "Gain 1 Fanatic Charge every second if you've Attacked in the past second" (ModCache.lua:15656, empty mods) and "Lose all Fanatic Charges on reaching Maximum Fanatic Charges" (ModCache.lua:17530 nil). PoB has no Fanatic charge at all (grep "Fanatic" in Modules/Classes finds only the buff); the buff exists only as the config box buffFanaticism (ConfigOptions.lua:1691-1692, shown when CanGainFanaticism is set, default off), which gives Speed MORE 75 for cast, Cost MORE -75 and AreaOfEffect INC 75 for cast (CalcPerform.lua:735-739, 75 scaled by buff effect on self and rounded down) and only when the main skill is self-cast: totems, traps, mines and triggered skills are excluded (CalcActiveSkill.lua:425-431), so a CoC build gets nothing. Wiki (Fanaticism rev 1699114, Zeal rev 1673787, patch 3.29.0): max 3 charges, 5 s buff, 75% less cost; an attack must be newly used each second, it need not hit, triggered attacks and proxies do not count, a channelled attack (Cyclone) adds no further charges, charges keep coming during the buff. Derived cycle (arithmetic, not verified in game): 3 charges take 3 s of attacking and the buff lasts 5 s, so continuous attacking can re-gain it before it ends; whether a re-gain refreshes the 5 s is unverified. Needs Virtue allocated first.
- Instruments of Virtue 19417 (feeds [[Damage calculation#More and less]] and [[Damage calculation#Flat damage]]): both lines parse. "10% more Attack Damage for each Non-Instant Spell you've Cast in the past 8 seconds, up to a maximum of 30%" is Damage MORE 10 with mod flag 1 = attacks, Multiplier CastLast8Seconds, limit 30 total (ModCache.lua:4010, ModParser.lua:2922-2924); nothing in PoB counts the casts, the only source is the config count multiplierCastLast8Seconds (ConfigOptions.lua:1519-1520, default 0, "only non-instant spells you cast count"), so a hand-set count of 3 or more gives the full 30%. Battlemage is flag Battlemage plus MainHandWeaponDamageAppliesToSpells MAX 100 (ModCache.lua:14358, ModParser.lua:5542); CalcOffence.lua:585-592 adds the main-hand weapon's damage as base spell damage (floor, spell flag), which needs a weapon in slot 1. Wiki (rev 1631183): triggered spells, totems, traps and mines do not count as casting. A hybrid attack plus spell build is the intended user; a pure spell build gets only Battlemage, a pure attack build only the 30% when it also casts. Hub of the Zeal and Justice branches. Do not confuse with the gem Battlemage's Cry ([[Warcries]]).
- Righteous Providence 53884 (feeds [[Critical strike chance]]; its minors add [[Critical strike multiplier]]): full. CritChance INC 1 per point of Str if Int is not higher, else per point of Int (two mods with PerStat plus condition IntHigherThanStr, ModCache.lua:3409; the condition is set at CalcPerform.lua:429 and 493), and +50 Str and Int (ModCache.lua:2273; tree.lua:16665 grantedStrength/grantedIntelligence 50). The 50 points also raise attribute requirements met by gear, so a spell build can drop attribute rolls on items; the crit line is therefore at least 50% increased once allocated. The two minors on the way (662, 37486) give +10% critical strike multiplier each (tree.lua:16627, 16646). Wiki (rev 1418617): same text, 3.13.0 rework.
- Inevitable Judgement 48214 (feeds [[Exposure]] and [[Damage calculation#Resistance, exposure and penetration]]): full. "Critical Strikes ignore Enemy Monster Elemental Resistances" is IgnoreElementalResistances under Condition CriticalStrike (ModCache.lua:14841); "Non-Critical Strikes Penetrate 10% of Enemy Elemental Resistances" is ElementalPenetration BASE 10 under the negated condition (ModCache.lua:18440). PoB runs a crit pass and a non-crit pass (CalcOffence.lua:3399 sets CriticalStrike = pass 1). In the crit pass useThisResist returns 0, so effectiveResist is 0 (CalcOffence.lua:3496, 3581-3591): resistance, exposure and penetration are all skipped, even a negative resistance, as the wiki says (rev 1490190: equal to overriding the enemy resistance to 0). The flag CannotElePenIgnore takes the first branch (CalcOffence.lua:3584) and beats it: it is not consumed here, so an item with that flag disables the crit half (unverified on a real build). Value depends on the enemy resistance and the crit rate: against an enemy with resistance above 0 the crit half acts as 100% penetration, against one at or below 0 (Exposure, curses) it removes the benefit of the negative resistance. Needs Righteous Providence allocated first.
- Augury of Penitence 40059 (feeds [[Damage calculation#Damage taken]] for the enemy side and [[Receiving damage#Damage taken modifiers]] for the incoming side): full. "Nearby Enemies take 16% increased Elemental Damage" is an EnemyModifier ElementalDamageTaken INC 16 (ModCache.lua:18392), added to takenInc for elemental hits (CalcOffence.lua:3533, 3562); "Nearby Enemies deal 8% less Elemental Damage" is an EnemyModifier ElementalDamage MORE -8 (ModCache.lua:18349), read in the enemy damage multiplier (CalcDefence.lua:1715, 1742, 1817). No config gate and no radius: PoB treats every enemy as nearby; wiki (rev 1418618) says the radius is 60. The 16% is additive with the other increased damage taken (Sanctuary's 15% and Bottled Faith), not a separate multiplier.

On-hit and when-hit sources of this ascendancy (rule from task 027): none is an on-hit chance. Sanctuary and Augury are standing effects, uptime = the enemy being inside the ground (Sanctuary) or inside 60 units (Augury, per the wiki); Fanaticism is a periodic buff (see the derived cycle above, PoB has no box for the charges and a plain tick for the buff); Virtue's 30% is a rolling 8 s window over non-instant, non-triggered spell casts, so it needs about 3 casts per 8 s (0.375 casts per second) for the full bonus. PoB's boxes: conditionEnemyOnConsecratedGround (default off), conditionStationary (0), buffFanaticism (off), multiplierCastLast8Seconds (0); none of them is derived from the build.
