Clear is how many enemies one cast reaches in a map: area radius for area skills, pierce, chain and fork for projectile lines, spread of several projectiles. PoB computes the numbers (radius, PierceCount, ChainMax, ForkCountMax) and prices none of the extra hits: pierce, chain and fork are worth +0 FullDPS (EK probe), the radius is worth +0 (Worb probe), and Concentrated Effect shows as pure damage (+39.3%) while the radius falls from 2.6 m to 2.1 m. A bonus per remaining chain or per pierced enemy is counted, with the lone-target defaults when the config is unset.
Order on a collision: Split, Pierce, Fork, Chain, Return, one behaviour per collision, so pierce delays fork and chain, and chain never fires on a lone boss. The single-target side (shotgun prevention, overlap, hit fraction) is in [[Projectiles hitting a single target]] and the return pass in [[Returning projectiles]]; this note is only about packs.
No community norm for a required clear level was found, so the generator can only guard the seed's own clear shape (labelled model). Lines PoB drops are in [[Clear lines PoB cannot value]].

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#Increased and reduced]], [[Damage calculation#More and less]] - radius and hit counts are not damage steps (they set how many enemies are hit, outside the per-hit order); only their damage side-effects act here (Chain Support less hit damage, Pierce Support more projectile damage, Concentrated Effect more area damage, per-remaining-chain and per-pierced increased damage)
