The Slayer ascendancy (tree group 517, start node 33795) is the Duelist's attack and leech ascendancy: seven notables in 3.29, each on its own arm of the start node (Endless Hunger sits behind Brutal Fervour, Headsman behind Bane of Legends). This note lists every notable with its tree text, its wiki rule where it adds something, how PoB parses it (live parse through PoB's own tree pipeline) and what it does in the one sample build, Slayer Ele Hit (Duelist/Slayer, level 95; it allocates Brutal Fervour, Masterful Form, Impact and Bane of Legends). Mechanics that have their own note are linked, not repeated: [[Leech]] (Brutal Fervour, Endless Hunger), [[Culling strike]] (Headsman), [[Stun]] and [[Bleed, poison and corrupted blood immunity]] (Endless Hunger), [[Endurance charges]] and [[Frenzy charges]] (Masterful Form), [[Impale]] (Bane of Legends' reflect line), [[Accuracy and hit chance]] (Impact), [[Fight length]].

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#More and less]] - Bane of Legends 10% and 20% more, Impact's up to 15% more by proximity, Headsman's cull threshold; [[Damage calculation#Hit chance]] and [[Damage calculation#Critical strike]] - Impact's 50% increased Accuracy Rating and Overwhelm's base crit and multiplier; [[Damage calculation#Hit rate]] - Endless Hunger's attack speed and Masterful Form's charges; [[Receiving damage#Recovery]] - Brutal Fervour and Endless Hunger leech; [[Receiving damage#Damage taken modifiers]] - Brutal Fervour's 10% reduced damage taken; [[Receiving damage#Immunity and avoidance]] - Endless Hunger's stun and bleed immunity while Leeching.

Follow-up sweeps on the same sample (same label): (1) Brutal Fervour's 12.2% DPS is the Leeching condition, not the node's own mods: with the node removed and conditionLeeching ticked FullDPS equals the base exactly (None 4,097,797.4; Pinnacle 3,286,350.5) while TotalEHP stays at the removed value (the -10% damage taken is the node's own line). The 'while Leeching' mods in this build are Hydrascale Gauntlets (Item:13) Damage INC 34, Onyx Amulet (Item:15) Damage INC 34, Tree:63727 Gladiator's Perseverance Damage INC 40 (Attack) and Tree:10143 DamageTaken INC -10. (2) Bane of Legends' '10% more Damage if you've Killed Recently' is not counted until conditionKilledRecently is ticked: FullDPS +9.85% (None 4,501,595.7) and +10.06% (Pinnacle 3,616,976.8); with Bane removed and the box ticked FullDPS equals the Bane-removed value without the box, so the whole 10% is Bane's in this build. (3) Impact distance, config meleeDistance versus the default 15: 30 units -7.27% (None) / -7.85% (Pinnacle); 40 units -12.36% / -12.68%. So the Impact row above assumes every target is inside 15 units.

On-hit and when-hit sources of this ascendancy (rule from task 027): Endless Hunger's overkill leech is "on a killing Hit", not a chance and not counted by PoB (line dropped). Brutal Fervour and Endless Hunger run on the Leeching condition: PoB output fields on the sample are Speed 7.8304 and HitChance 100 (None) / 98 (Pinnacle) and LifeLeechDuration 9.71 s with the node, so a leech instance is renewed far more often than it expires while the character hits; PoB does not model the gaps (see conflicts). Headsman's two speed buffs are 20 s timers after a kill of a Rare or Unique (attack speed) or any enemy (movement speed): their boxes are conditionKilledUniqueEnemy (ConfigOptions.lua:1642) and conditionKilledRecently (:1281), both unticked in the sample; Bane of Legends' 10% needs a kill within the 'recently' window (reminder text tree.lua:59604; the duration is not stated in the evidence). Impact's AoE per enemy killed recently needs multiplierKilledRecently (:1284). With the vault 'Chance on hit uptime' model, p = 1 for every kill/hit condition above; the number of kills per second is not in PoB output, so those uptimes are NOT VERIFIED as numbers.
