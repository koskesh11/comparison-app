Assassin's Mark curses ONE enemy so that hits against it have added crit chance and added crit multiplier, and its death gives the killer a power charge. At full strength it is what lifts the Cast on Critical Strike attack to 100% crit in both seeds (EK 93.23 to 100, FR 98.43 to 100, see [[Critical strike chance]]), and it is worth +51% (EK) and +11% (FR) CombinedDPS in PoB. PoB treats it as a permanent, always-applied curse: it does not model WHEN the Mark is on (both seeds apply it only through a Rare or Unique trigger, so trash has no Mark), the power charge on kill, or the quality charge chance. Related: [[Curse limit]], [[Curse application and effect]], [[Power charges]], [[Critical strike multiplier]].

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#Critical strike]] - the Mark's added crit chance joins the base before the increased pool; its added multiplier joins the extra-damage sum; [[Damage calculation#Increased and reduced]] - the added chance is multiplied by the increased crit pool (EK 2.77 x 8.53), so Mark effect and crit chance scale each other.
