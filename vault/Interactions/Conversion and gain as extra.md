Conversion moves a share of one damage type to a LATER type (Physical, Lightning, Cold, Fire, Chaos; never backwards); gain-as-extra adds a copy and leaves the source whole. Both act on hits only, early: after flat and added damage, before increased and more. The converted or gained part keeps the modifiers of every type it passed through, once each, so physical increased still helps damage that ends up as cold. The order itself is in [[Damage calculation]] (Conversion, Gain as extra: wiki quotes and PoB file:line live there; not repeated here). This note holds what the order note lacks: providers, the traps, and what PoB leaves out. None of the three seed builds has any conversion or gain in PoB (probe 2026-10-25: conversionTable empty for every active skill of CoC EK Assa, Worb and FR CoC v4).

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#Conversion]] and [[Damage calculation#Gain as extra]] - the type of damage is decided here, before increased and more, so every later step (the history-based increased pool, resistance, which ailments can apply) works on the final types, not on the gem's printed type.
