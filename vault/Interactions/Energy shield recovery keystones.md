Four keystones reshape how a character gets energy shield back, and each one trades a PoB-visible number for another. Zealot's Oath moves life regeneration onto ES (the standard partner of [[Chaos Inoculation]]); Ghost Reaver turns life leech into ES leech and doubles the ES leech cap but switches ES recharge off; Divine Shield caps recoverable ES at Armour and regenerates 3% of recently prevented physical damage as ES per second; Wicked Ward keeps a started recharge running through damage for 4 s but cuts the recharge rate by 40%. PoB prices three of them well and the fourth only by its downside, so a PoB-only search never picks Wicked Ward and picks Divine Shield on a zero-armour ES build as if nothing happened to the pool. Related: [[Leech]] (cap and instances), [[Recovery]] and [[Energy shield and hybrid]] (written in another lane, resolve at merge).

Pipeline: [[Defences#Recovery and leech]] - regen, recharge and leech are the over-time recovery side that sits next to the ES pool, and Divine Shield's cap changes the pool itself (CalcDefence.lua:2593-2594 adds EnergyShieldRecoveryCap, not maximum ES, to every TotalPool); see also [[Receiving damage#Recovery]].

Without Oath: regen 115/s (-2,135.6/s, -94.9%), recharge unchanged, life regen 0.1/s. Divine Shield: cap = Armour 0, PhysicalTotalPool 17,117 -> 1, TotalEHP 273,342 -> 0 (Pinnacle) and 102,318 -> 5 (None); regen 2,250.6 and recharge 11,876 stay unchanged in PoB. Wicked Ward: recharge 11,876 -> 7,125 (x0.6). Ghost Reaver + Wicked Ward: recharge 0, Wicked Ward adds nothing.
- EK (ES 5,189, Armour 658, recharge 1,989/s, delay 2.0 s, ES leech 1,491.96/s of which 973.06 instant, cap 518.9). Ghost Reaver: recharge 1,989 -> 0, cap 518.9 -> 1,037.8, ES leech rate 1,491.96 -> 2,010.86 (+518.9, +34.8%); no life leech source, so only the doubled cap counts.
