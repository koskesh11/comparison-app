The Templar's Hierophant ascendancy has eight notables in 3.29 plus eight minor nodes and the class start 30940 (17 nodes with ascendancyName Hierophant, tree.lua:15635-15960 and the start at 15960). Every notable hangs on its own arm of the start node, so each arm is a separate build choice: Totems (start - 26714 - Pursuit of Faith - 14870 - Ritual of Awakening), Arcane Surge (start - 44797 - Arcane Blessing - 33167 - Illuminated Devotion), Brands (start - 38387 - Sign of Purpose), Mana (start - 11046 - Divine Guidance - 29994 - Sanctuary of Thought) and Charges (start - 22637 - Conviction of Power). Reading the connections in tree.lua, the arms with a second notable are strict chains: Illuminated Devotion needs Arcane Blessing, Ritual of Awakening needs Pursuit of Faith, Sanctuary of Thought needs Divine Guidance (29994 links only to 922 and 29026). Deallocating the first notable of a chain would therefore also drop the minor behind it and the second notable (PoB DeallocNode, Classes/PassiveSpec.lua:830-842; derived from the tree.lua links, not run). Ascendancy nodes have no attribute requirement and no mastery. No seed is a Hierophant, so seeds: [] and nothing here was measured in a headless run: not measured, no seed of this class. Every figure below is read from PoB source or the wiki.

The new in 3.29.0: Ritual of Awakening gives 3% more Damage per Summoned Totem, down from 5% (wiki Hierophant rev 1675191 version row 3.29.0; tree.lua:15664). Older guides still quote 5%.

Other notes own the mechanics, this note only says which notable feeds which one: [[Totems]] (Pursuit of Faith, Ritual of Awakening, the totem half of Arcane Blessing), [[Arcanist Brand]] (Sign of Purpose is a brand-frequency notable; brand mechanics in general are not covered here), [[Arcane surge]] (Arcane Blessing, Illuminated Devotion and the two minors on their path), [[Endurance charges]] and [[Power charges]] (Conviction of Power), [[Receiving damage]] (Divine Guidance mana before life), [[Skill cost from ES]] (the mana-to-ES conversion of Sanctuary of Thought sits next to the ES-pays-mana keystones). No Hierophant line touches [[Consecrated ground]] (the 17 nodes were all read; none names consecrated ground).

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#More and less]] (Ritual of Awakening 3% more per totem, Arcane Blessing more spell damage, Sign of Purpose more activation frequency), [[Damage calculation#Increased and reduced]] (Transfiguration of Mind and the Arcane Surge area bonus), [[Damage calculation#Hit rate]] (totem placement speed, brand activation), [[Receiving damage#Mana before life]] (Divine Guidance), [[Receiving damage#Energy shield]] (Sanctuary of Thought), [[Receiving damage#Recovery]] (mana regeneration, totem and player life regeneration).

What the minor nodes give (tree.lua stats; ModCache lines 4966, 6484, 6559, 6624, 11618 all parse): 44797 and 33167 20% increased Mana Regeneration Rate and 20% increased Effect of Arcane Surge on you each; 38387 20% mana regeneration and 14% increased Brand Damage (ModCache.lua:4966, keywordFlags 1048576); 26714 and 14870 20% mana regeneration and 20% increased Totem Placement speed; 11046, 29994 and 22637 8% increased maximum Mana and 20% mana regeneration. The 8% maximum Mana nodes also feed Transfiguration of Mind (below).

Arcane Surge arithmetic in PoB (CalcPerform.lua:712-731): effect = 1 + (sum of ArcaneSurgeEffect and BuffEffectOnSelf INC)/100, so with minor 44797 alone 1.2 and with 44797 + 33167 (the path to Illuminated Devotion) 1.4. Mana regeneration INC = 30 x effect, cast speed INC = 20 x effect, and the Arcane Blessing damage is a separate MORE mod of 20 x effect on Spell damage (Condition AffectedByArcaneSurge), i.e. 24% with effect 1.2 and 28% with 1.4. It is a different term from the 35% more of the Arcane Surge support (sup_int.lua:234-235 carries its own Damage MORE mod; only ArcaneSurgeDamage is scaled by effect), so the two multiply. Under the bare config the buff is 100% on or off (see [[Arcane surge]]).

Transfiguration of Mind worked from the tree alone (no other source assumed): path to Divine Guidance holds 11046 (8%) and Divine Guidance (30%), Mana INC 38, so floor(38 x 0.3) = 11% increased Damage; adding 29994 gives 46, floor 13.8 = 13%; the Conviction arm's 22637 adds 8 more, 54 x 0.3 = 16.2, floor 16%. Gear and other tree mana increases add to the sum. PoE and PoB round down.

On-hit and when-hit sources of this ascendancy (rule from task 027): one. Arcane Blessing's "Gain Arcane Surge when you or your Totems Hit an Enemy with a Spell" is not a chance: every spell hit grants the buff (no p). Duration 4 s (reminder text on the minor node 44797, tree.lua:15644-15646; the buff itself is described in [[Arcane surge]]). Hit rate would come from PoB's HitSpeed (else Speed) times HitChance of the spell skill that hits, or for a totem the totem's hit rate; no seed here, so not measured. With the vault "Chance on hit uptime" model and p = 1, the buff is up whenever a spell hit lands at least once every 4 s (HitSpeed x HitChance >= 0.25 per second; a hit on any enemy counts), and below that rate the uptime is 4 s x hits per second. Value when up: the numbers above (cast speed, mana regeneration and Spell MORE at 20 x effect, and the Illuminated Devotion lines). PoB's config box: the condition is set without a box for a non-totem hit spell main skill, while a totem main skill needs conditionTotemsHitSpellRecently ticked (ConfigOptions.lua:1306-1307, default off) because of the name mismatch below; the buffArcaneSurge box (ConfigOptions.lua:1013) is the fallback and turns the buff on for the whole fight. Sign of Purpose is the other timed effect: it is a share of the brand's attached duration (the last 25%), see Sign of Purpose in conflicts for how PoB scales it.
