Skills can be made to reserve life instead of mana (and to cost life instead of mana, which is a separate flag). Reserved life is unavailable for the rest of the session, so unreserved life shrinks, and at 50% or less unreserved life the character is on Low Life, which switches on low-life payoffs (Pain Attunement, Bloodthirst) and lets a build stack life reservation on top of mana reservation. The price: hits and leech only see the unreserved part, PoB lets life reservation go past 100% without complaint, and PoB computes leech from maximum life although the game discards leech instances at full unreserved life. Lifetap (support) only changes costs, it does not reserve; Arrogance is the reservation support.

Pipeline: [[Receiving damage#Life]] and [[Receiving damage#Recovery]] - hits are removed from the unreserved life that is left, and leech/recovery stops at full unreserved life, so reserved life changes both the pool that dies and the pool that refills
