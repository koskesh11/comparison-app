The Witch's Necromancer ascendancy has eight notables in 3.29 plus eight minor nodes and the class start 60791 (17 nodes with ascendancyName Necromancer, tree.lua:57307-57647). A ninth node tagged Necromancer, Nine Lives 27602 (tree.lua:89983-90001), has no links at all. It is not on the tree and exists only for the matching Forbidden Flame / Forbidden Flesh pair (see below).

The five arms off the start are separate build choices: Mindless Aggression (start - 26298 - Mindless Aggression 54159), which branches into Unnatural Strength (54159 - 39818 - 65153) and Bone Barrier (54159 - 55646 - 14603); Corpses (start - 60547 - Plaguebringer 11490 - 23509 - Corpse Pact 23572); Offerings (start - 18309 - Mistress of Sacrifice 48719); Essence Glutton (start - 18574 - 3554); Commander of Darkness (start - 5415 - 36017). Read from the tree.lua links (undirected): Corpse Pact needs Plaguebringer, and both Unnatural Strength and Bone Barrier need Mindless Aggression. Deallocating Plaguebringer or Mindless Aggression would drop the nodes behind it (PoB DeallocNode, Classes/PassiveSpec.lua:830-842; derived from the links, not run). Ascendancy nodes have no attribute requirement and no mastery. No seed is a Necromancer (EK is Assassin, FR is Occultist, Worb and Fireball are Elementalist), so seeds: [] and nothing here was measured in a headless run: not measured, no seed of this class. Every figure below is read from PoB source or the wiki.

New in 3.29: nothing. The wiki version history for the class ends at 3.27.0 (Corpse Pact 4% increased Attack and Cast Speed per corpse, previously 2%, wiki Necromancer rev 1677399); the 3.24.0 row reworked Mindless Aggression (20% more Maximum Life, 10% more Damage) and Bone Barrier (40% of Life as Extra ES, 1% of minion damage leeched to you). Older guides still quote the pre-3.24 numbers (the old Bone Armour grant, the Recovery Rate per minion line and the extra 20% minion Life on Bone Barrier are gone). Bone Barrier's 1% physical reduction per minion is in the 3.29 tree but is not named in the wiki's version rows.

Other notes own the mechanics, this note only says which notable feeds which one: [[Minions]] (every "Minions have / deal" line of the class; PoB computes ONE minion actor, the main skill's minion, CalcPerform.lua:3492-3495), [[Spectre]] and [[Golem buffs]] (spectres and golems are minions and take the MinionModifier lines; no Necromancer line names a golem, so the golem buffs on the player are unchanged), [[Aura effect and aura count]] (Commander of Darkness and the 5% node 5415), [[Wither]] and [[Chance on hit uptime]] (Unnatural Strength's Withered chance), [[Shock]] and [[Chill]] (Corpse Pact), [[Conversion and gain as extra]] (Unholy Might's 100% physical to chaos), [[Leech]] (Bone Barrier), [[Receiving damage#Recovery]] and [[Forbidden Flame and Flesh]] (Nine Lives recoup). No line touches [[Consecrated ground]] or [[Totems]].

Transfer check from the tree alone: an Essence Glutton or Plaguebringer build gains nothing in PoB at default settings, because the count of nearby corpses is 0 until the box is filled in (ConfigOptions.lua:1136, default 0); at 10 nearby corpses Essence Glutton reaches its caps (2.0% of ES per second and 50 Mana per second) and Plaguebringer's 10% more damage and 10% reduced enemy damage need only one corpse. Deciding the corpse count is a scenario assumption, not a PoB output.

On-hit and when-hit sources of this ascendancy (rule from task 027): one hit-based chance. Unnatural Strength grants minions Unholy Might: 100% physical converted to chaos and 25% chance to apply Withered on Hit (reminder text on the node, tree.lua:57588-57607; wiki Unholy Might rev 1702379). Source line: "Minions have Unholy Might". Hit rate would be the minion's own hit rate from PoB's minion output (HitSpeed or Speed times HitChance of the minion's attack or spell, per minion; the number of minions multiplies it, see [[Minion alive count]]); no seed here, so not measured. Duration: each Withered stack lasts 2 s and is not refreshed by a new stack, up to 15 stacks, 6% increased chaos damage taken each (vault [[Wither]], wiki Withered rev 1702206). Uptime with the vault "Chance on hit uptime" model: stacks S = min(15, 0.25 x hits per second on ONE target x 2 s) summed over all minions, so one minion hitting twice a second keeps 1 stack and the cap needs 30 hits per second across all minions. Value when up: 6 per stack as Chaos Damage Taken on the enemy (PoB WitherEffectStack = floor(6 x (1 + MinionWitherEffect / 100)) when the minion skill carries CanWither, CalcPerform.lua:2756-2763), so it only helps chaos damage and the physical to chaos conversion above makes all of the minion's physical damage chaos, which is what lets it use the stacks. PoB's config boxes: the node sets the flag by itself (no tick needed); the stack count box multiplierWitheredStackCount is shown when Condition:CanWither exists (ConfigOptions.lua:1839, default 0, so the debuff is not counted unless you fill it in), and buffUnholyMight / minionbuffUnholyMight (ConfigOptions.lua:1019, 1023) are the manual twins that duplicate the node. Corpse Pact's chill and shock are not chance-on-hit: they apply to enemies near a corpse you spawned in the last 4 s.
