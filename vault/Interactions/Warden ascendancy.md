The Warden ascendancy is called "Raider" in PoB's tree data (tree.lua ascendancyName and ascendancy id Raider, class name Warden in PoB's class list, start node 58427) and Warden on the wiki since 3.25.0 (wiki Warden rev 1677394: "In version 3.25.0, the Warden ascendancy class replaced the Raider"; the page Raider is a redirect, rev 1536168). It is the Ranger's elemental-ailment and tincture ascendancy with ten notables in tree.lua: nine can be allocated (Oath of Summer, Oath of Spring, Oath of Winter, Avatar of the Wilds, Mother's Teachings, Lesson of the Seasons, Enduring Suffusion, Experienced Herbalist, Seasoned Hunter) and Fury of Nature 18054 is an orphan with no connections (see its provider row). No sample build is a Warden (grep of the user's builds for ascendancy Raider and Warden found no file), so nothing here was measured in a headless run: not measured, no sample of this class. Every PoB figure is a live parse or read from source; every game rule is a wiki quote. Text changes in 3.28.0 (wiki Warden version history): Lesson of the Seasons went from -15 fire, cold and lightning damage taken per Bark to -25 of each type, and Avatar of the Wilds from 80% to 100% more Elemental Damage; the wiki's 3.29.0 Ascendancy Changes lists no Ranger ascendancy (Version 3.29.0 rev 1699085; 3.29.x hotfixes not checked). Mechanics with their own note are linked, not repeated: [[Ignite]], [[Freeze]], [[Shock]], [[Shatter]], [[Scorch, Brittle and Sap]], [[Elemental ailment count]], [[Enemy ailment threshold]], [[Spell suppression]], [[Flat physical damage reduction]], [[Chance on hit uptime]], [[Defences]]. Barkskin, Unbound Avatar and tinctures have no note yet; they are described here.

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#Ailments from hits]] (Oath of Summer turns ignite into scorch, Oath of Spring stacks shock, Oath of Winter freeze floor and Hoarfrost, Avatar of the Wilds forces freeze, shock and ignite), [[Damage calculation#More and less]] (Avatar of the Wilds 100% more Elemental Damage while Unbound), [[Damage calculation#Damage taken]] (shock and scorch change what the enemy takes), [[Receiving damage#Damage taken modifiers]] (Lesson of the Seasons: flat damage taken from spell hits per Bark), [[Receiving damage#Spell suppression]] (Lesson of the Seasons: prevented suppressed damage per missing Bark); tinctures (Experienced Herbalist, Enduring Suffusion, Seasoned Hunter) are item-effect nodes and not a damage step.

What PoB leaves out (game rules from the wiki that PoB does not count): Oath of Winter's "Enemies you Freeze remain Frozen for at least 2 seconds" is unparsed (grep of ModParser.lua and Calc*.lua for a freeze minimum finds nothing; [[Freeze]] already records it), only the Hoarfrost flag is parsed and its effect is the config HoarfrostStacks x 20% freeze duration (ConfigOptions.lua:1915-1916; CalcOffence.lua:5408); Avatar of the Wilds' Unbound Fury gain is not modelled (the gain line is dropped, "Cannot gain while Unbound" is unparsed) so Unbound is the checkbox "Are you Unbound?" Oath of Spring: whether the enemy-side shock effect is clamped to 2% by ShockMax OVERRIDE 2 is unverified (ShockMax is read at CalcPerform.lua:889 for self-shock; the generic ailment loop was not read).

Hand arithmetic from the quoted rules (not a PoB result, not a game test): Avatar of the Wilds needs 100 Unbound Fury and gains at most one per ailment type per 0.2 s, so at most 5 per second per type; with all three types 15 per second gives 100 in 6.7 s, with one type 20 s; no gain while Unbound (10 s), so the best-case uptime is 10 / (6.7 + 10) = 59% for three types and 10 / (20 + 10) = 33% for one (rounded down). Lesson of the Seasons at 10 Bark takes 250 off each damage type per spell hit before suppression.

On-hit and when-hit sources of this ascendancy (rule from task 027): no seed or sample, so no hit rate was read from a PoB output field. (1) Avatar of the Wilds "Gain 1 Unbound Fury when you inflict an Elemental Ailment with a Hit": not a chance, one fury per ailment type per 0.2 s at most, only ailments you inflict yourself (wiki Unbound Avatar rev 1649442: not minions, totems, traps or mines); the Unbound state lasts 10 seconds; value when up: 100% more Elemental Damage and hits always inflict freeze, shock and ignite; PoB's config box "Are you Unbound?" is off by default. (2) Oath of Winter "Hits that fail to Freeze due to insufficient Freeze Duration inflict Hoarfrost": every such hit, no chance; Hoarfrost lasts 10 s and adds 20% freeze duration per stack, removed when frozen (wiki Oath of Winter rev 1632609); PoB box HoarfrostStacks, default 0. (3) Oath of Summer and Oath of Spring have no chance line (every hit that would ignite scorches; shocks stack to 50 at a maximum 2% each); the stack counts are the PoB boxes ScorchStacks and ShockStacks (placeholder 1). Uptime for the stack nodes follows the vault "Chance on hit uptime" model once a hit rate exists.
