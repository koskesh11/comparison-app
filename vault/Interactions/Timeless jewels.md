A timeless jewel conquers every tree passive in its radius: the seed on the jewel plus the conqueror name decide, per node, whether it is replaced or gets an extra line, and each conqueror turns every keystone in radius into one fixed new keystone. PoB does this exactly from its own lookup tables (LUT) for all five jewels, so the stat changes are in its numbers; what PoB misses is a few lines of the new nodes, and a plain "remove the jewel" test is misleading when the jewel converted an allocated keystone.

EK Militant Faith (item 3, socket 61419, Large radius, seed 8587, High Templar Dominus): 97 tree nodes in radius (22 notables, 4 keystones, 71 small), 9 allocated: four Intelligence travel nodes become +10 Devotion each, four more allocated nodes get +5 Devotion, and the allocated keystone Bitter Frost (12128, "Cannot deal non-Cold Damage") becomes Inner Conviction (3% more Spell Damage per Power Charge). Devotion is 60. Removing the jewel in memory takes FullDPS from 15,136,639 to 0, because the plain tree node Bitter Frost is back; with Bitter Frost deallocated the build is 12,475,646, so the jewel is worth +21.3% FullDPS here. Allocating a keystone inside the radius is how a build obtains the conqueror keystone (PassiveSpec.lua:1357-1364 replaces every keystone in radius): EK spends the Bitter Frost point to get Inner Conviction. The two per-Devotion lines alone do not change damage: they lower the mana cost of the main skill from 22 to 18 and add 10.3 mana per second recovery.

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#More and less]] - Inner Conviction is its own more multiplier (3% x 7 Power Charges); the replaced and added node stats otherwise act in the step of their own line, and the Devotion lines act on cost and regeneration, not on the damage pipeline
