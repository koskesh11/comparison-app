Critical strike chance is the chance a hit becomes a crit: (base + additional) x (1 + increased) x more, rolled once per skill use. Base comes from the weapon for attacks (its local crit, 8.5% on the Skean bases of both seeds) and from the gem for spells (Ethereal Knives 10%). PoB caps the result at 100 (CritChanceCap), then applies luck, bifurcation, and for attacks multiplies by the accuracy hit chance. The generator must reach 100% on a Cast on Critical Strike attack (rule [[CoC requires 100% crit and hit]]); in both seed builds the pre-cap chance is about 115 to 117%, but without the curse on the enemy (Assassin's Mark) it falls to 93.23% (EK) and 98.43% (FR), so the Mark, not gear alone, closes the gap. Related: [[Lucky critical strikes]], [[Power charges]], [[CoC trigger]], [[Critical strike multiplier]].

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#Critical strike]] - the chance is decided before the multiplier is applied; [[Damage calculation#Hit chance]] - attacks roll accuracy a second time for the crit; [[Damage calculation#Increased and reduced]] and [[Damage calculation#More and less]] - increased is one additive pool, more (for example Mystical Infusion 30% less) multiplies after it.
