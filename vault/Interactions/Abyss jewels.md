Abyss jewels are jewels with their own mod pool, socketed into Abyssal Sockets on a weapon, helmet, gloves, body armour, boots or belt (up to 6 per item; the item must say "Has N Abyssal Socket(s)") or into a passive-tree socket. The base picks the pool: Murderous (melee), Searching (ranged), Hypnotic (caster), Ghastly (summoner); life, mana, ES, resistances, attributes and CDR roll on all four. The host item's "% increased Effect of Socketed Abyss Jewels" multiplies every value on its jewels and rounds down, but not jewels on the tree.
EK: Darkness Enthroned (2 sockets, 97% effect) holds two rare Hypnotic Eyes; Grim Iridescence's "2% increased Cooldown Recovery Rate" becomes 3 and carries CDR 50 (Like Clockwork) over the 52% CoC breakpoint (FullDPS -41.0% without it). Worb: one magic Hypnotic Eye in a rare Stygian Vise, a life stick (+39 life; its Phasing line does nothing in PoB). The mod values are plain item mods; the value lies in which family the generator picks per build. Items with a CDR line of their own are listed in [[Cooldown recovery breakpoints]]; this note holds only the abyss-jewel CDR prefixes.

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#Flat damage]], [[Damage calculation#Hit rate]] - added Spell damage lines act in the flat step, a CDR line acts through the cooldown (trigger cap = hit rate of the triggered spell, see [[CoC trigger]] and [[Cooldown recovery breakpoints]]); life, ES and mana lines act on the defence side, not in the outgoing order
