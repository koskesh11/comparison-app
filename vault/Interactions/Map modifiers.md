Map modifiers are area-wide changes to monsters and to the player (more monster damage, lower maximum resistances, less recovery, no regeneration, less cooldown recovery, player curses, Thorns on Rare monsters). PoB models 31 of them through two sets of four dropdowns (MapPrefix1-4, MapSuffix1-4) plus 13 "Player is cursed by" counters, all as flat config values: nothing in the app decides which mods a map has, and PoB has no "can this build run this mod" check. The size of a mod is read from its tier (Red / Yellow / White) and the roll is always the top of the range; "% increased effect of map mods" scales the dropdown mods but not the curses (wiki: map curses are not scaled).
The measured deltas below were taken on the three seeds with every dropdown at the Red tier and the seed's own config (Pinnacle boss, resistance penalty -60). Several mods hurt a seed far more than others: Toughness cuts crit damage dealt (-32 to -36% DPS on all three), Fatigue halves the CoC trigger rate on EK and FR, Rust removes block, Hexproof removes the seed's own curses on monsters.
The 3.29.0 rework replaced the old "monsters reflect 13-18%" mods by fixed Thorns on Rare monsters (400/600/800 physical and 900/1200/1500 elemental by tier). PoB still lists the old percentages, applies nothing, and does not offer them in the dropdown, so reflect damage taken is invisible in every PoB number.
