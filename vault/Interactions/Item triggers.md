Four unique items trigger the spells socketed in them: Cospri's Malice (cold spell on melee crit), Mjölner (lightning spell on hit, no mana cost), The Poet's Pen (spell after every wand attack) and Kitava's Thirst (50% chance when a skill costs at least 100 mana). Each trigger has its own cooldown of 0.25 s (0.1 s for Kitava), so the rate cap is far below Cast On Critical Strike's (see [[Trigger rate cap and cooldown]] for the rounding rule; this note does not repeat it). The point of these items is a free trigger source without a gem slot or a mana multiplier, but the triggered spell sits in the item sockets (no 6-link, no Spell Echo-style support beyond the sockets).

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#Hit rate]] - the item decides how often the socketed spell lands (hit rate of the triggered spell); the item's own gem-level and damage lines act at the stages the lines name.
