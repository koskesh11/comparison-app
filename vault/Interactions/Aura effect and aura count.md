An aura has no slot limit in the game or in PoB; what limits it is reservation (see [[Reservation and reservation efficiency]]). What the build can steer is how strong each aura is on the player: "increased effect of Non-Curse Auras" and "Auras from your Skills have X% increased Effect on you" add up in one increased pool, "more Aura Effect" lines (Supreme Ego) multiply, and Generosity trades the self effect for ally/minion effect. PoB counts how many auras and heralds affect you automatically (no config tick), so "per Aura or Herald affecting you" damage works without setup.
Generosity is an INCREASED aura effect in PoB and in the wiki, not a "more" multiplier (it adds to the same pool as Sovereignty etc.), and at gem level 20 it gives 39%, at the level cap 40 it gives 54%.

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#Increased and reduced]], [[Damage calculation#More and less]] - aura effect scales the aura's own modifiers (a single INC pool of AuraEffect, BuffEffect, AuraEffectOnSelf ... plus a separate MORE), before those modifiers enter the build's damage and defence sums
