The Trickster ascendancy (start node 55236) is the Shadow's evasion plus energy shield ascendancy: seven notables and seven small nodes (15 nodes with the start, the same node ids in the 3_26, 3_27, 3_28 and 3_29 trees). This note lists every notable with its 3.29 tree text, its wiki rule, how PoB parses it (live parse through PoB's own tree pipeline: 6 of 7 grade full, Spellbreaker partial) and what it does in three sample builds. Mechanics that have their own note are linked, not repeated: [[Leech]] and [[Recovery]] (Soul Drinker, Polymath's recover on kill), [[Spell suppression]] (Spellbreaker), [[Frenzy charges]] (Swift Killer), [[Chill]] and [[Freeze]] (One Step Ahead), [[Energy shield and hybrid]] and [[Skill cost from ES]] (Escape Artist), [[CoC trigger]] (samples T1 and T2), [[Defences]]. The Scion's Ascendant tree can reach reduced Trickster options, see [[Ascendant ascendancy]]. The graph and point costs of the branches were not part of the evidence and are not listed here.

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#More and less]] - Polymath's 2% more Damage per mastery type; [[Receiving damage#Energy shield]] - Escape Artist's evasion and energy shield conversion by gear, Soul Drinker's energy shield leech; [[Receiving damage#Damage taken modifiers]] - Heartstopper's less damage taken from hits and over time; [[Receiving damage#Spell suppression]] - Spellbreaker; [[Damage calculation#Hit rate]] - One Step Ahead's action speed floor (a floor under chill and freeze only); [[Receiving damage#Recovery]] - Polymath's recover on kill and Soul Drinker's leech.

Ghost Shrouds are not on the Trickster tree: they sit on the keystone Ghost Dance 35255 (tree.lua:79895-79901), and the Trickster notables lost their Ghost Shroud lines in 3.16.0 (wiki Escape Artist rev 1536935); PoB's box for them is useGhostShrouds (ConfigOptions.lua:942) and neither T1 nor T3, the two samples that allocate Ghost Dance, ticks it.

Small nodes (live parse, all 7 full): each gives 14% increased Evasion Rating and 5% increased maximum Energy Shield plus one extra line - 10% increased Damage (13219), 4% increased Attack and Cast Speed (35598), 4% increased Movement Speed (19587, 63908), 15% increased Frenzy Charge Duration (37191), 40% increased total Recovery per second from Energy Shield Leech (2336, read only with energy shield leech: Soul Drinker supplies it), 10% increased Energy Shield Recharge Rate (58454).

Patch history that matters for any number (3.27.0, wiki per-notable rows; PoB TreeData 3_28 and 3_29 carry the new text, 3_26 the old): One Step Ahead 'at least 108% / at most 92%' became 90% / 90%; Heartstopper 40% became 50%; Spellbreaker lost '+20% chance to Suppress Spell Damage' and went from +10% to +15% prevented; Soul Drinker lost '20% increased Attack and Cast Speed while Leeching Energy Shield'. Unchanged since: Polymath (2% more, 3.26.0), Escape Artist (4 per 1 and 1 per 8, 3.26.0), Swift Killer (3.19.0). Two of the three samples (T1, T2) are saved on the 3_26 tree and PoB converted them in memory to 3_29 with no node lost or gained; their file shows the old, higher numbers (converted T1 FullDPS 14,046,328.5 against 17,306,659.4 native, -18.84%; converted T2 2,139,208.9 against 2,413,450.6, -11.36%), so never read an old-tree number as the current value of One Step Ahead or Soul Drinker.

Parse and gates (live parse, b5b-pca-asc.md Trickster section):
- Escape Artist 29825 (tree.lua:84237, name 84239): '+4 to Evasion Rating per 1 Maximum Energy Shield on Equipped Helmet' and '+1 to maximum Energy Shield per 8 Evasion Rating on Equipped Body Armour'. Both lines apply as PerStat mods read from the equipped item's base values (CalcDefence.lua:483-506); gate = gear (an ES helmet, an evasion body armour). Wiki: the bonus is outside item-defence scaling, but modifiers on the item's base defence raise it. Whether PoB reads the base or the locally modified value was not checked.
- Heartstopper 28884 (tree.lua:84197): 'Every 10 seconds: take 50% less Damage from Hits for 5 seconds; take 50% less Damage over Time for 5 seconds'. All mods carry a condition; PoB sets it only from the list box buffHeartstopper (ConfigOptions.lua:1468-1474, default None = the node does nothing; Average = -25% hits and -25% over time, Hit = -50% hits all the time, Damage over Time). The Average mode is the expectation; Hit is an upper bound because the hits land in the protected half of the cycle only half the time. No sample sets the box.
- One Step Ahead 23225 (tree.lua:84081): 'Your Action Speed is at least 90% of base value; Nearby Enemy Monsters' Action Speed is at most 90% of base value'. The player floor changes a number only when action speed is below 0.9: the boxes conditionChilled (ConfigOptions.lua:1198) and conditionFrozen (:1204), Temporal Chains. The enemy half has no reader that changes FullDPS or TotalEHP (CalcPerform.lua:1143-1148; the only other reader is the enemy stun duration text, CalcOffence.lua:5585-5590).
- Polymath 55867 (tree.lua:84157): '2% more Damage for each different type of Mastery you have Allocated' plus recover 1% of Life, Energy Shield and Mana on Kill for each type. One MORE mod of 2% x N, not 1.02^N (verified chain: ModParser.lua:1462 gives Multiplier:AllocatedMasteryType, counted in CalcSetup.lua:705-729, 787-788). N is a tree-search parameter. The recover-on-kill lines are per-kill amounts; PoB has no kill rate, so they are in no FullDPS or TotalEHP number.
- Soul Drinker 48999 (tree.lua:84000): '2% of Damage Leeched as Energy Shield; Energy Shield Leech effects are not removed when Energy Shield is Filled'. The flag CanLeechEnergyShieldOnFullEnergyShield makes the conditions Leeching and LeechingEnergyShield true automatically (CalcPerform.lua:910-913, like Brutal Fervour for life, :906-909): every 'while Leeching' mod in the build (gear, tree, other ascendancy) is on with no leech source.
- Spellbreaker 41891 (tree.lua:84040): 'Prevent +15% of Suppressed Spell Damage while on Full Energy Shield' applies as SpellSuppressionEffect with Condition FullEnergyShield; the box conditionFullEnergyShield (ConfigOptions.lua:186, default off) is the only setter, no automatic one exists (grep of Modules/Calc*.lua, Classes/, Data/*.lua finds none; compare FullLife, CalcPerform.lua:86). It reaches damage only through a suppression chance from gear or tree (CalcDefence.lua:1147; the max-hit multiplier needs 100% effective chance, :2072, 2075; average multiplier :2816-2825). Line 2 '50% chance for Energy Shield Recharge to start when you Suppress Spell Damage' is DROPPED: it parses to a junk EnergyShield BASE 50 mod with leftover text (Data/ModCache.lua:10068) and PoB ignores the line, so recharge starting on suppress is not modelled. It is a chance when you suppress (when-hit class); PoB has no spell hit rate input for it and none was read, so no per-scenario uptime is computed.
- Swift Killer 32947 (tree.lua:84139): '200% increased Charge Duration; +2 to Maximum Frenzy Charges'. The +2 counts only with the box useFrenzyCharges (ConfigOptions.lua:906; overrideFrenzyCharges :909; CalcPerform.lua:1058-1059), then the build sits at maximum charges. The 200% duration is display-only (CalcPerform.lua:988-1002); charge generation and charge uptime are not modelled.

T1's Forbidden Rite loses -16.32% instead of -16.67% (cause not traced). T3's count of 11 is 8 ordinary mastery types plus the 3 Runegraft masteries (Treachery, the Agile, the Fortress) counted as 3 types; the wiki Polymath page (rev 1537561) says runegrafted masteries count as ONE type. If the wiki is right in 3.29, PoB over-counts T3 by 2 types = 4 points of more damage (-15.25% on removal instead of the measured -17.86%, evidence arithmetic). NOT verified in game.

Escape Artist is a defence node in all three samples and, in T1 only, a damage node: ES 11,307 falls to 9,867 and Evasion 33,881 to 31,404 when its mods go, and Forbidden Rite adds a share of current ES to its chaos damage (Data/Skills/act_int.lua:7409 per the evidence, 'life * lifeDealtAsChaos + energyShield * energyShieldDealtAsChaos'), so the ES from Escape Artist is damage on that one skill; Cyclone does not move, and Ice Spear (T2) and Frost Blades (T3) have no ES scaling (FullDPS 0.00%). Inputs: helmet ES 143 / 411 / 24 and body-armour evasion 3,093 / 3,552 / 3,834 (T1 / T2 / T3), so T3's bonus is small on the evasion side. PoB's evasion is an average chance, the game uses an entropy counter (vault gap 'Evasion entropy').

Soul Drinker and One Step Ahead lost their value in 3.27.0: on the converted 3_29 tree both measure 0.00% (Soul Drinker's only offence line, the 20% attack and cast speed while leeching ES, is gone; One Step Ahead's floor is now 90% and ActionSpeedMod is 1 in all three samples). On the old 3_26 tree the same nodes were worth a lot (T1: One Step Ahead -7.32%, Soul Drinker -12.26% FullDPS; T2: Soul Drinker -11.36%); those numbers are the history of the old text, not the current value. What is left of Soul Drinker is the automatic Leeching condition, the leech that survives full ES, and the 2% leech; recovery is not in TotalEHP (vault gap 'EHP ignores recovery').

On-hit and when-hit sources of this ascendancy (rule from task 027): no Trickster notable carries a chance-on-hit line. The only chance line is Spellbreaker's 50% chance to start Energy Shield Recharge when you suppress a spell hit (dropped by PoB, not modelled); its trigger rate would be the enemy spell hit rate times the suppression chance, neither was read from the samples, so no per-scenario uptime number is given and the vault [[Chance on hit uptime]] model was not applied. Heartstopper is a fixed 10 second cycle (5 s hits, then 5 s over time), not a chance. Polymath's recover on kill is per kill at 100%, with no kill rate in PoB; see [[Fight length]] for the kill-count question.
