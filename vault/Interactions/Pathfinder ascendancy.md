The Pathfinder ascendancy (PoB class Ranger, ascendancy id Pathfinder, start node 64111; wiki Pathfinder rev 1677395, version-history rows 3.21.0 and 3.4.0) is the flask ascendancy and has seven notables in 3.29: Master Toxicist, Master Distiller, Nature's Reprisal, Nature's Adrenaline, Nature's Boon, and the two behind another notable, Master Surgeon (behind Nature's Adrenaline) and Master Alchemist (behind Nature's Boon). Tree text is the same in 3_28 and 3_29 for all but Master Toxicist ("nearby Enemies are Poisoned" in 3_27, "Enemies within 1.5 metres" from 3_28, matching the wiki; b1d-taar-ranger.md section 0). The wiki's 3.29.0 Ascendancy Changes lists no Ranger ascendancy (Version 3.29.0 rev 1699085; 3.29.x hotfixes not checked). Mechanics with their own note are linked, not repeated: [[Flask uptime]] (the charge model all flask lines need), [[Offensive utility flasks]], [[Defensive utility flasks]], [[Life and mana flasks]], [[Penetration]], [[Conversion and gain as extra]], [[Channelling]] (Master Distiller excludes channelled skills), [[Wither]], [[Poison]], [[Elemental ailment immunity]], [[Chance on hit uptime]], [[Damage over time multiplier]].

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#Critical strike]], [[Damage calculation#Resistance, exposure and penetration]] and [[Damage calculation#Gain as extra]] (the three Master Distiller bonuses act at those steps: 250% increased crit chance, 25% elemental penetration, 37% of physical as extra chaos), [[Damage calculation#Damage taken]] (Nature's Reprisal: Withered raises the chaos damage the enemy takes), [[Damage calculation#More and less]] (Master Toxicist's 20% more poison damage), [[Receiving damage#Recovery]] (Master Surgeon's life flask recovery and Nature's Boon's flask effects); flask charge supply (Nature's Adrenaline, Master Alchemist) is not a damage step, it only decides how often the flask effects above are up ([[Flask uptime]]).

Graph and point cost (PoB `node.pathDist` after SelectAscendClass on a 3_29 spec, b1d-taar-ranger.md section 0; each unallocated node is one point, cumulative from the start node, so the second notable's figure contains the first). The ascendancy budget is 8 (wiki Ascendancy class rev 1697030, quoted in b1-tree-ascendancy-analyst.md).

Reading the rows: Nature's Reprisal's -19.15% is the +50% Withered effect at the hand-set 15 stacks: the skill Toxic Rain of Withering already sets Condition:CanWither (Data/Skills/act_dex.lua:15120-15121), and PoB's stack effect is floor(6 x 1.5) = 9% increased chaos damage taken per stack (CalcPerform.lua:2756-2762, 845-848). PoB drops the 25% chance and the 2 s duration. Nature's Adrenaline and Master Surgeon do nothing in damage or EHP because their value is flask uptime and life recovery, which PoB shows only in the flask tooltip (ItemsTab.lua:4790-4857) and in output stats (the FlaskChargeGen keys come from a block that runs in breakdown mode, CalcPerform.lua:1613-1634). "No change in PoB" is not "no value". Nature's Boon scales only magic utility flasks (CalcPerform.lua:694, 1602-1606): the unique Amethyst (Progenesis) and the life flask are not scaled. Not allocated in the sample, so no measurement: Master Distiller, Master Toxicist, Master Alchemist.

What PoB leaves out (each a game rule that PoB does not count): Master Distiller's charge cost (3 charges per use from each of the three flask types) is not counted, PoB gives 100% of the three bonuses whenever a Diamond, a Bismuth and an Amethyst flask are in a flask slot (Condition:HaveXFlask, CalcSetup.lua:1029, set whether or not the slot is active); its bonuses are tagged "not Triggered, not Channel", so a CoC-triggered spell never gets them in PoB; Master Alchemist's 50% not-consume line and Nature's Adrenaline's charge generation reach only the tooltip and output stats, never DPS or EHP (grep of Modules/Calc*.lua finds no other reader of FlaskChanceNotConsumeCharges; CalcSections.lua:1783 is display); Master Alchemist's ailment removal, Master Toxicist's poison spread on kill and Master Surgeon's "do not queue" are unparsed.

Master Distiller arithmetic (not a PoB result): on the sample, Speed 5.274 uses per second x 3 charges = 15.8 charges per second per flask type, against FlaskChargeGen 1 and UtilityFlaskChargeGen 2 per second before crit and kill charges; the timed supply covers about 6% and 12% of that (rounded down), so the bonuses cannot be up all the time without kills or crit charges. Wiki Master Distiller rev 1592668: charges are consumed from all three flask types whenever any non-channelling skill is used, even if the skill cannot hit; consumption scales with "Flask Charges used" mods; partial charges can be consumed; the bonus covers the whole use of the skill. The uptime formula is in [[Flask uptime]] (its `model:`); this note adds the drain term 3 x uses per second per flask type, which PoB's tooltip does not include (ItemsTab.lua:4830-4857 covers flask duration only).

On-hit and when-hit sources of this ascendancy (rule from task 027): (1) Nature's Reprisal "25% chance to inflict Withered for 2 seconds on Hit": hit rate would be PoB's Speed (5.274 per second) times the hits per target per use, which is unverified (pods overlap model not run); duration 2 s per stack; with the vault "Chance on hit uptime" model, expected stacks are about p x H x d = 0.25 x H x 2 = 0.5 x H for H hits per second on the target, so 15 stacks need H of 30 or more on one target (arithmetic); value when up 9% increased chaos damage taken per stack; PoB's config box multiplierWitheredStackCount is ticked at 15 (hand-set) in the sample, so -19.15% is an upper bound until H on one boss is modelled, and the Withered stacks of the gem itself add to it. (2) Master Toxicist "Poisons you inflict during any Flask Effect have 20% chance to deal 100% more Damage": PoB takes the average, Damage MORE 20 for Poison while Condition:UsingFlask (set automatically by an active flask, CalcPerform.lua:1776-1778); the poison hit rate was not measured (not allocated in the sample). (3) Master Toxicist's kill line and Master Alchemist's flask-use cleanse are not hit effects.
