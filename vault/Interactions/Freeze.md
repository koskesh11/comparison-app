Freeze is a hit-only cold ailment: the enemy gets 100% less action speed for 0.06 s per 1% of its ailment threshold dealt by one hit (max 3 s, discarded under 0.3 s). Crits inherently freeze. PoB computes chance and the duration modifier but never sets the enemy Frozen condition, so every "vs Frozen" payoff shows 0 until conditionEnemyFrozen is ticked. Bosses keep a 50% action-speed floor (3.25.0): they still count as Frozen for conditions, but it is a slow, not a lock.

Lead rulings encoded here (2026-10-24): for a boss, `conditionEnemyFrozen` is set only when the model below gives a duration of at least 0.3 s (Worb.xml 0.533 s: yes; day-1 snapshot 0.191 s: no). Unique bosses cannot shatter. Berek's Pass "+5000 Armour while Frozen" is a self-state line, not a payoff. Foulborn The Three Dragons mutations replace lines; the cold line stays unless mutated. Providers PoB leaves unparsed say so in `text`. Paths in `source` are relative to the PoB install unless a URL is given. See [[Chill]], [[Shatter]], [[Enemy ailment threshold]], [[Damage type can ailment]], [[Elemental ailment count]], [[Own shock effect]], [[Foulborn The Three Dragons]].
