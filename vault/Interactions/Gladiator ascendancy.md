The Gladiator ascendancy (tree group 351, start node 32730) is the Duelist's block, bleed and weapon-type ascendancy: seven notables in 3.29, after the 3.25.0 rebalance that replaced the old list (wiki Gladiator rev 1675934 version history: removed Painforged, Reigning Veteran, Violent Retaliation, Outmatch and Outlast, Blood in the Eyes and Arena Challenger; added Determined Survivor, More Than Skill, Jagged Technique, War of Attrition, Measured Retaliation and Weapon Master). Older guides quote the removed notables. No sample build is a Gladiator: nothing here was measured in a headless run, so there is no measured table; every figure is the tree text, the wiki quote or PoB source. Mechanics that have their own note are linked, not repeated: [[Block]] (Determined Survivor, More Than Skill), [[Bleed]] and [[Crimson Dance]] (Jagged Technique, Gratuitous Violence), [[Leech]] (Weapon Master claw line), [[Fight length]] (War of Attrition's ramp), [[Damage calculation]].

Pipeline: [[Receiving damage#Block]] - Determined Survivor replaces the shield's block value and More Than Skill makes the block roll lucky; [[Damage calculation#More and less]] - War of Attrition's 1% more per second and Weapon Master's more lines; [[Damage calculation#Ailments from hits]] - bleed chance and Jagged Technique's Aggravate; [[Damage calculation#Hit rate]] - the doubled dual-wield attack speed; [[Receiving damage#Recovery]] - Weapon Master's instant leech with a claw.

Graph and point cost (tree.lua `in`/`out` lists, group 351; the cap is 8 ascendancy points, Modules/Build.lua:902). Notables with a second one behind them are strict chains.

Small nodes (tree.lua stats): 3651 5% increased Attack Speed and 10% increased Damage with One Handed Weapons; 1675, 24538 and 37623 5% increased Attack Speed and +3% Chance to Block Attack Damage each; 48760 and 33179 5% increased Attack Speed and 'Attacks have 15% chance to cause Bleeding' each; 14726 10% increased Attack Damage and 5% increased Attack Speed. The two small nodes on the way to Jagged Technique (48760, 33179) give the bleed chance; the notable itself gives no chance.

On-hit and when-hit sources of this ascendancy (rule from task 027): the 15% chance to cause Bleeding on 48760 and 33179 is on hit; Jagged Technique changes the bleed that results, it is not a chance. Measured Retaliation's lines act on Retaliation skills that are Usable or used (what makes such a skill Usable is not quoted in the evidence: NOT VERIFIED here; wiki Retaliation rev 1625957 is the rule source). No sample, so there is no PoB hit rate (Speed, HitChance) to put through the vault 'Chance on hit uptime' model: the uptimes are NOT VERIFIED as numbers, and the only config boxes involved are conditionBlockedRecently (More Than Skill) and conditionEnemyBleeding (Gratuitous Violence), both unticked by default.
