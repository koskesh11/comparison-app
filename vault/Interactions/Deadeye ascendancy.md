The Deadeye ascendancy (PoB class Ranger, ascendancy id Deadeye, start node 56856; wiki Deadeye rev 1677393, last version-history row 3.25.0) is the projectile ascendancy and has eight notables in 3.29: Avidity, Gathering Winds, Occupying Force, Focal Point, Ricochet, Far Shot, and the two that sit behind another notable, Wind Ward (behind Gathering Winds) and Endless Munitions (behind Ricochet). The tree text is the same in tree versions 3_27, 3_28 and 3_29 (b1d-taar-ranger.md section 0), and the wiki's 3.29.0 Ascendancy Changes lists no Ranger ascendancy (Version 3.29.0 rev 1699085; 3.29.x hotfixes not checked). Mechanics with their own note are linked, not repeated: [[Frenzy charges]] (Avidity), [[Curse limit]] and [[Assassin's Mark]] (Focal Point), [[Projectile hits single target]] and [[Returning projectiles]] (Endless Munitions, Ricochet), [[Spell projectile supports]], [[Clear - chain, pierce, fork and area]], [[Chance on hit uptime]] (the stack-uptime method used for Gale Force), [[Receiving damage]] (Wind Ward and Focal Point damage taken). Tailwind and Gale Force have no note of their own yet; they are described here.

Pipeline: [[Damage calculation#Hit chance]] (Avidity: 5% more Accuracy Rating per Frenzy Charge), [[Damage calculation#Hit rate]] (Gathering Winds: Tailwind raises action speed, so uses per second), [[Damage calculation#More and less]] (Far Shot's distance ramp is a more multiplier; Endless Munitions and Ricochet change how many projectiles and targets are hit, which PoB's TotalDPS does not use), [[Receiving damage#Damage taken modifiers]] (Wind Ward 4% less damage taken per Gale Force, Focal Point 25% less damage taken).

The ascendancy budget is 8 (wiki Ascendancy class rev 1697030, quoted in b1-tree-ascendancy-analyst.md).

Isolated runs (only the notable's own modList emptied, path nodes stay): Gathering Winds -10.60% and Focal Point -14.45% as above, Endless Munitions 0.00%, Ricochet 0.00% FullDPS and 0.00% EHP with ChainMax, ChainRemaining and ChainMaxString 1 to 0. So Ricochet's own value is not in PoB's damage: chains are used only through "per remaining chain" mods (Modules/ModParser.lua:1558-1559, 2650-2652; CalcOffence.lua:1068-1078). "No change in PoB" is not "no value": Endless Munitions and Ricochet are worth what the projectile and chain notes say, not what PoB shows. Not allocated in the sample, so no measurement: Avidity, Occupying Force, Far Shot, Wind Ward (an allocation what-if costs ascendancy points and was not run). Hand arithmetic from the PoB lines: Far Shot at the placeholder distance of 40 units is 30 x (40-35)/(70-35) = 4.29% more (ramp {35 to 0, 70 to 1}, ModStore.lua:598-612, pca section 6); with the sample's 70 units it is the full 30%. Wind Ward at 10 Gale Force is 0.96^10 = 0.665, a 33% reduction, in PoB permanently.

What PoB leaves out (each is a game rule from the wiki that PoB does not count): Gale Force is never generated, the count is the config multiplierGaleForce (default 0; the sample hand-sets 10); Wind Ward's "Lose all Gale Force when Hit" is unparsed, so PoB credits the reduction permanently (the downside is the point of the node: every hit resets it); Focal Point's 25% less damage taken is parsed with leftover text and dropped (Classes/PassiveTree.lua:862), so TotalEHP does not change, and its mark transfer is unparsed; Endless Munitions and Ricochet are 0 in PoB DPS; Far Shot's no-spread line, Occupying Force's two placement lines and Avidity's charge gain are unparsed.

On-hit and use-triggered sources of this ascendancy (rule from task 027): Gathering Winds "Gain 1 Gale Force when you use a Skill" is on skill use, not a chance; each stack lasts 4 seconds, maximum 10, and gaining one does not refresh the others (wiki Gathering Winds rev 1463616). The use rate is PoB's Speed field (5.368 per second on the sample). Model (arithmetic from the wiki rule and that field, not a PoB result): 10 stacks after 10 uses, 10 / 5.368 = 1.86 s, rounded down 1.8 s; all 10 are kept while the gap between uses stays below 4 s - 9 / 5.368 s = 2.3 s; below 2.5 uses per second the full 10 are never held. Value when up: Tailwind is 8% increased Action Speed (wiki Tailwind rev 1493255) and the node adds 10% increased effect per Gale Force, which PoB computes as ActionSpeed INC floor(8 x (1 + 100/100)) = 16 at 10 stacks (CalcPerform.lua:767-769; probe on the sample); that is the sample's -10.60% FullDPS. PoB's config box: multiplierGaleForce (ConfigOptions.lua:936-937), hand-set 10 in the sample, so PoB assumes the maximum permanently. Wind Ward's reset is a when-hit source: hits taken per second come from the enemy, not from a PoB output field. Avidity's charge is one per second while moving, so its uptime follows the movement time of the build, not a hit rate.
