# 005 Audience: the owner; publishing is open

- Date: 2026-10-26 (grilling rounds 1-2), round 18 and round 20 (2026-10-27)
- Decided by: owner
- Status: accepted, one question open
- Scope: product, docs, the repo's visibility

## Context
The first project was aimed at the PoE community. In round 1 the owner dropped that audience.

## Decision
- The app is built for the owner. The docs are written for the owner and Claude, and readable by any developer.
- No human reviewer exists ("I have nobody participating in this project"); never plan around a developer friend.
  Cold reviews are done by a fresh AI reviewer (007).
- Platform: Windows and the browser.
- Open: round 2 said "private for good"; round 18 said the owner presents the app to the community when it is good
  enough ("I don't want to be called a shit developer again"). The steps before going public stay planned (GitHub
  checks, history clean-up, cold review); the decision is the owner's.

## Consequences
- Code quality is held to a public standard even while the repo is private (006).
